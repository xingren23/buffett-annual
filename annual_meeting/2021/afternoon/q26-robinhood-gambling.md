## 问题

股东问如何看待 Robinhood 等交易 App 以及金融科技公司，让各个年龄、各种经验的人都能参与股市。

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特说很期待读 Robinhood 的 S-1，重点是看它在声称不收客户任何费用的情况下，如何描述自己的收入来源。
2. 他提到 Robinhood 的参与者中约 12%—13% 在交易看跌和看涨期权，并说自己查过 Apple 未平仓的 7 天期与 14 天期看涨期权数量。
3. 他认为这类交易不违法也谈不上不道德，但「我不认为你会围绕人们做这种事来建设一个社会」；美国企业既是存钱的好地方，也造出了极好的赌博筹码。
4. 芒格说这简直是「对着公牛挥红旗」，认为吸引文明人和正派公民来投资「从根本上就是错的」。
5. 两人把各州彩票与 Robinhood 相提并论：巴菲特说各州是在「对希望征税」，芒格说各州取代黑手党成了赌数字这行的东家。
6. 巴菲特回忆父亲霍华德·巴菲特在国会时，众议院办公大楼里就有收赌注的跑腿人。

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Becky Quick**（01:19:13）：这个问题来自内布拉斯加州的 Robert Miles。「关于交易 App。Robinhood 和其他交易 App，或者说金融科技公司，让各个年龄、各种经验的人都能参与股市，你们怎么看？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:28）：（笑）嗯，我很期待读一读 Robinhood 的 S-1，那就是你要发行证券时向 SEC 提交的那份大文件，而且，它，你知道，它已经成为过去一年半里加入股市的那个赌场板块，赌场集团，里非常重要的一部分。我确实要说，我关心的是，当他们说自己不收客户任何费用时，他们怎么处理收入来源。我是说，你知道，看他们怎么描述这一点会很有意思。不过，但他们吸引了，也许本来就打算吸引，但他们确实吸引了，我记得读到过，他们赌场参与者里有 12% 或 13% 在交易（期权）看跌和看涨合约。我去查了 Apple，你知道，未平仓的 7 天期和 14 天期看涨期权有多少。我敢肯定其中很大一部分是通过 Robinhood 来的。

那就是一大群人在押注，他们在赌 Apple 未来 7 天或 14 天的股价。这没有什么，你知道，这不违法，也谈不上不道德。但是，我不认为你会围绕人们做这种事来建设一个社会。我是说，如果我们一群人落到一座荒岛上，而且知道永远不会获救，我还是其中一员，我说，那好，我在这边开个交易所。（笑）我来交易我们的玉米期货之类的，我想，一个非常富裕的社会能在多大程度上奖励那些懂得如何利用，不只是美国公众，而是全世界公众，的赌博本能的人，你知道，这可不是成就里最值得称道的部分。不过我认为美国取得的成就整体上还是相当值得称道的。（笑）而且我认为，实际上，你知道，美国企业已被证明是人们存放资金、储蓄的好地方。但它们同时也造出了极好的赌博筹码。

而如果你在人们口袋里第一次有了钱的时候去迎合这些赌博筹码，告诉他们一天可以做 30 笔、40 笔、50 笔交易，而且你不收他们任何佣金，但你卖的是订单流之类的东西，那，我希望这种事别再多了，我就这么说吧。我会很想读读那份招股书。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:22:36）：嗯，这可真是对着公牛挥红旗。（笑声）我觉得，这样的东西居然能吸引文明人和正派公民来投资，简直糟透了。这从根本上就是错的。我们不想靠卖对人有害的东西赚钱。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:05）：可各州还在用彩票干这事呢，你知道。

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:08）：不，但那也很糟。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:09）：是啊，我知道。我明白，可我是说——

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:11）：那非常糟。那非常糟——

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:12）：一旦你——

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:13）：这正是它的问题之一。做这些事正在变得体面起来。各州跟 Robinhood 一样糟糕。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:23）：嗯，某种意义上他们更糟。我是说，他们实际上是在对——征税

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:27）：我知道——我知道，我知道。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:29）：是啊。他们在对希望征税。

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:33）：不止如此——

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:34）：而且他们从我跟查理这儿收到的税，也没比从——多到哪儿去——

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:38）：美国各州取代了黑手党，成了赌数字这行的东家。事情就是这样。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:49）：是的。

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:49）：他们把黑手党推到一边，说这是我们的生意，不是你们的。这没法让我为自己的政府感到骄傲。

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:55）：我小时候，我父亲（霍华德·巴菲特）在国会。众议院办公大楼里居然有个收赌注的跑腿人（笑），真的。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:19:13）：This question comes from Robert Miles in Nebraska. "The trading apps. What do you think about Robinhood and other trading apps, or fintech companies, enabling all ages and experience to participate in the stock market?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:28）：(Laughs) Well, I'm looking forward to reading the S-1 on Robin — that's the big thing you file with the SEC when you're going to be offering securities., and — It's — you know, it's become a very significant part of the casino aspect — of the casino group — that has joined into the stock market in the last year or year and a half. And I do want to say I'm concerned of how they handle the source of income when they say they don't charge the customer anything. I mean, you know, I'm — it'll just be interesting to watch how they describe it. I mean, but — But they — they have attracted — maybe set out to attract — but they have attracted, I think I read where 12 or 13% of their casino participants were dealing in (option) puts and calls. I looked up on Apple, you know, the number of seven-day calls and 14-day calls outstanding. And I'm sure a lot of that is coming through Robinhood. And that's a bunch of people writing — they're gambling on the price of Apple over the next seven days or 14. There's nothing, you know — there's nothing illegal about it. There's nothing immoral. But — I don't think you'd build a society around people doing it. I mean, if a group of — a group of us landed on a desert island, and we knew we would never be rescued, and I was one of the group, and I said, well, I'll set up the exchange over here. (Laughs) And I'll trade our corn futures and everything around — I think — I think the degree to which a very rich society can reward people who know how to take advantage, essentially, of the gambling instincts of the — not only the American public — worldwide public — it's, you know, it's not the most admirable part of the accomplishment. But I think what America's accomplished is pretty admirable overall. (Laughs) And I think actually, you know, American corporations have turned out to be a wonderful place for people to put their money and save. But they also make terrific gambling chips. And if you cater to those gambling chips when people have money in their pocket for the first time, and you tell them they can make 30 or 40 or 50 trades a day, and you're not charging them any commission, but you're selling your order flow or whatever, it — I hope we don't have more of it, I'll put it that way. And I will be interested in reading the prospectus. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:22:36）：Well, that is really waving the red flag at the bull. (Laughter) I think it's just God-awful that something like that would draw investment from civilized men and decent citizens. It's deeply wrong. We don't want to make our money selling things that are bad for people.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:05）：But we've got the states doing it with the lottery, you know.

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:08）：No, but that's bad, too.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:09）：Yeah, I know. I understand, but I'm saying —

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:11）：That's very bad. That's very bad —

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:12）：Once you —

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:13）：That's one of the things that's wrong with it. It's getting respectable to do these things. The states are just as bad as Robinhood.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:23）：Well, in a sense they're worse. I mean, they're really taxing the —

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:27）：I know it's — I know, I know.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:29）：Yeah. They're taxing hope.

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:33）：Not only that —

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:34）：And they don't get much in the way of taxes from me and Charlie than they do —

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:38）：The states in America replaced the Mafia as the proprietor of the numbers game. That's what happened.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:49）：Yep.

**Charlie Munger**（01:23:49）：They pushed the Mafia aside and said that's our business, not yours. Doesn't make me proud of my government.

**Warren Buffett**（01:23:55）：When I was a kid, my dad (Howard Buffett) was in Congress. They had a numbers runner in the House office building (laughs), actually.
