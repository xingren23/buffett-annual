## 问题

股东问伯克希尔怎么看量化投资：Jim Simons 的 Medallion 基金三十年来费后年化 39%，证明这套方法有效。他还问伯克希尔是否会聘请一位量化方面的副手，与泰德·康姆斯或托德·韦施勒共事。

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特对第二个问题直接回答「不」，把量化本身的问题交给芒格。
2. 芒格说那家量化基金在短线交易上极为成功，靠的是有预测价值的小算法；只要还管用、钱还不断流入，他们就一直做。
3. 但用同一套体系机械地做长期股票预测，业绩远不如前；短线上做得太多反而会毁掉自己的优势，所以赚钱有上限。
4. 芒格称 Jim Simons 这些人非常聪明、非常富有，而且层次极高。
5. 巴菲特说伯克希尔不靠交易股票赚钱，「我们不认为自己知道该怎么做」，也不放心让别人代做。

## 关键引述

> **“they destroyed their own advantage”**
>
> 「就会毁掉自己的优势。」

> **“we're not trying to make money trading stocks”**
>
> 「但我们不是在靠交易股票赚钱。」

> **“if we knew how to make a lot more money trading stocks, we'd probably be trading stocks, too”**
>
> 「如果我们知道怎么靠交易股票赚多得多的钱，我们大概也会去交易股票。」

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:29:10）：This question comes from Bijay Koirala. "What do you think of quants? Jim Simon's Medallion Fund has done 39% net of fees for three decades, which proves that it works. Will you consider hiring a quant lieutenant in Berkshire to work alongside with Ted (Combs) or Todd (Weschler)?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:29:28）：Well, I'll say no to the second part, and I'll let Charlie handle the first part. (Laughter)

**Charlie Munger**（01:29:39）：Well, that's rather interesting. The (unintelligible) quant fund did fabulously on the short-term trading bit, they found little algorithms that worked to make them — they had predictive value. And as long as they kept working, they just kept doing it, as long as the money kept coming in. When they got to using the same system just to finding some little algorithm and trying to do it mechanically for long-term stock predictions, the record was not nearly as good. And in the short-term stuff, they found that if they tried to do it too much, they destroyed their own advantage. So, there was a limit on the amount they could make.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:15）：But they were very, very smart.

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:16）：Yes, they got very rich.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:18）：Very, very smart.

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:18）：Very smart, and very rich, yes.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:20）：And —

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:21）：And very high grade, by the way.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:23）：Yeah.

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:23）：Jim Simons.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:24）：But we're not trying to make money trading stocks. I mean — (Laughs)

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:29）：No.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:29）：The answer — we don't think we know how to do it. I mean, it isn't — if we knew how to make a lot more money trading stocks, we'd probably be trading stocks, too. But we don't know how to do it. And we really don't trust anybody else to do it for us. That simple.
