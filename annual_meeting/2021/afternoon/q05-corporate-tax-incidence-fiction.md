## 问题

25% 到 28% 的企业税率会怎样影响伯克希尔的各个子公司？

## 回答要点

1. 芒格认为 25%—28% 的企业税率不会是「世界末日」，伯克希尔适应过各种税率。
2. 巴菲特把联邦政府比作持有「AA 类股」：只分利润，不拥有资产，也不投票决定谁来经营。
3. 税率从 21% 升到 25%、28%，甚至回到他起家时的 52%，政府这只「AA 类股」都更值钱。
4. 「加税会全部转嫁给消费者」只在公用事业这类受监管业务成立，在伯克希尔多数业务里是「公司式的虚构」。
5. 高税率确实伤害伯克希尔股东，巴菲特认为这也许是相当合理的，但否认转嫁说法讲得通。
6. 他建议政府可设「伯克希尔·哈撒韦税务公司」把未来税收证券化，并称这是联邦政府未报告的资产。

**Becky Quick**（00:09:26）：还有一个问题。杰克·罗宾斯（Jack Robbins）问：「25% 到 28% 的企业税率会怎样影响伯克希尔的各个子公司？」

**Charlie Munger**（00:09:37）：嗯，我不认为那会是什么世界末日。不管税率是多少，我们都已经适应了。

**Warren Buffett**（00:09:42）：对。我想说，如果他们提高税率，那就等于联邦政府拥有这门生意的更大比例。我不是在说税率应该是多少。但我们有 A 类股和 B 类股。美国政府拥有一种我称之为 AA 类股的东西。这是一种非常特别的股票：他们拿走一部分利润，但不拥有资产，也不投票决定谁来经营这家公司，或者别的什么。但如果政府想拿，我刚开始做生意的时候，联邦政府拿走公司利润的 52%。他们拥有，你愿意出多少钱买政府那部分 A 类，AA 类股？如果美国财政部公开发行，说这个载体，给它起个像 SPAC 或者更性感的名字，它只做一件事：永久拥有伯克希尔·哈撒韦未来的纳税额。这只股票现在值多少钱？它会支付大额现金股息，而且随着我们留存收益、把公司做大，它还会涨。

那么，如果税率是 25%、28% 或 52%，它比 21% 时更值钱。他们拥有的是一只特殊的股票。人们说这一切都会转嫁给客户，在公用事业业务里，确实如此，那是特殊情况。但在我们大多数业务里，并不是这样。当他们发表声明说这对你们所有人都会很糟糕（笑），如果我们缴更多税，那只是公司式的虚构。税率更高会伤害伯克希尔的股东，这也许是相当合理的，但说不是这样，就根本讲不通。我倒很想看到政府真的发行，他们本可以成立一家公司，就叫「伯克希尔·哈撒韦税务公司」，它拿走我们每年缴的全部税款。他们能把这个资产卖多少钱？人们谈论政府未备资金的负债，那其实是联邦政府一项未报告的资产：他们拥有伯克希尔的一部分，而且由他们决定占多少。

这是个有趣的问题。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（00:09:26）：One more question for you. Jack Robbins asks, "How will a 25 to 28% corporate tax rate affect Berkshire's companies?"

**Charlie Munger**（00:09:37）：Well, I don't think it would be the end of the world. We've adapted to the tax rate, whatever it is.

**Warren Buffett**（00:09:42）：Yeah. I would say, if they raise the tax rate, they're owning a — the federal government's owning a larger percentage of the business. I'm not — but I'm not saying what the tax rate is. But we have a Class A stock and a Class B stock. The U.S. government owns what I call the Class AA stock. And it's a very special stock. They get a percentage of the earnings, but they don't own the assets. And they don't vote on who gets to run the place or anything else. But if the government wants to take — when I was first starting, they used to take 52%, the federal government did, of corporate profits. And they've got — what would you pay to own the government's Class A — double-A — stock? If there was a public issue by the U.S. Treasury and they said this vehicle — give it a name like SPAC or something even sexier, but — and all it will do is it owns the future tax payments of Berkshire Hathaway forever. And how much is that stock now worth? And it gets — and it'll pay a big cash dividend, and they'll go up as we retain earnings and build the company and everything else. Well, it's worth more if it's — if the tax rate is 25% or 28% or 52%, than at 21%. They own a special stock. And when people talk about how it all gets passed through to the customer and everything — in the utility business, it actually does. That's a special case. But it doesn't — it doesn't in most of our businesses. I mean it's just — it's a corporate fiction when they put out statements about the fact that this will be terrible for all of you people who have — (laughs) — if we pay more taxes. It hurts the Berkshire shareholders if rates are higher. And that may be quite appropriate. But to say otherwise is just — it doesn't make any sense. I would love to see the government actually issue — they could have — I mean they could set up a company. Just call it the Berkshire Hathaway Tax Company. And it would take all the taxes we paid every year. How much would they be able to sell that asset for? They talk about unfunded obligations of the government. That's an unreported asset of the federal government. They own part of Berkshire, and they get to determine how much. I mean, it's an interesting question.
