## 问题

为什么 GEICO 和 BNSF 的利润率明显低于主要竞争对手 Progressive 和 Union Pacific？现任管理层能否至少做到持平？

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特先答铁路：看第一季度数字，伯克希尔与 Union Pacific 的对比已经「好了不少」。
2. 他称赞 Katie Farmer 在 BNSF 干得非常出色。
3. 五年或十年后 BNSF 与 Union Pacific 谁盈利更高，会是个有趣的问题：过去伯克希尔盈利更高，后来被 Union Pacific 超过。
4. 双方都认为自己拥有略好一点的经营特许权；伯克希尔规模更大、业务量更多，「应该」多赚一点，但过去几年没有做到。
5. 他对这条铁路的结论是「相当不错的铁路」，感觉很好；GEICO 与 Progressive 的对比留到下一问。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:23:22）：This question comes from Glenn Greenberg. He says — it's on the profitability of GEICO and BNSF. He said, "Why do these companies operate at meaningfully lower profit margins than their main competitors, Progressive and Union Pacific? Can we expect current managements to at least achieve parity?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:24:13）：Was it GEICO and — ?

**Becky Quick**（01:24:14）：BNSF.

**Warren Buffett**（01:24:15）：Oh. Actually, if you look at the first quarter figures, you'll see that the Berkshire Hathaway/Union Pacific comparison has gotten (laughs) quite better. Katie Farmer's doing an incredible job at BNSF. And it'll be an interesting question, whether five years from now or ten years from now, BNSF or Union Pacific has the higher earnings. We've had higher earnings in the past. Union Pacific passed us. The first quarter, you can look at, and, you know — they think they've got the — a slightly better franchise. We think we've got a slightly better franchise. We know we're larger than Union Pacific. I mean, we will do more business than they do. And we should make a little more money than they do, but we haven't in the last few years. But, it's quite a railroad. I feel very good about that.
