## 问题

伯克希尔成功的要素之一，是巴菲特与芒格之间牢固的纽带——因为有了彼此，他们把公司管得更好。作为下一代的领军人物，阿贝尔与贾因彼此如何互动？又会向哪些能干的伯克希尔经理人请教？

## 回答要点

1. 贾因直言巴菲特与芒格的关系独一无二、无法复制，他和阿贝尔做不到，也想不出还有哪几对能复制。
2. 两人相识多年：每季度通电话互相通报各自业务，季度中间遇到问题就直接打电话给对方。
3. 阿贝尔说，看到不寻常的事就会打给贾因，确认做法是否稳妥、是否符合伯克希尔的思路。
4. 两人都强调这种互动不止于业务，还在于维护并延续伯克希尔的特殊文化。
5. 阿贝尔称贾因经营保险业务「没有人比他做得更好」，并称每天都享受与他共事。

## 关键引述

>
> 「每个季度我们也会交换笔记。」

> **“there's no one better at it”**
>
> 「没有人比他做得更好。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

**Becky Quick**（01:19:44）：这个问题来自 Jason Plawner。他说：「贾因先生、阿贝尔先生，这个问题是给你们的。伯克希尔成功的要素之一，是巴菲特先生和芒格先生之间牢固的纽带——因为有了彼此，他们把公司管得更好。你们两位显然是伯克希尔·哈撒韦下一代的领军人物，能不能讲讲你们彼此如何互动，或者你们会向哪些同样极为能干的伯克希尔经理人请教？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:11）：这是问谁的？

**Becky Quick**（01:20:13）：格雷格和阿吉特——

**Charlie Munger**（01:20:14）：阿吉特。

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:15）：好。

**Ajit Jain**（01:20:16）：嗯，毫无疑问，沃伦和查理的关系是独一无二的，不会被复制，至少我和格雷格做不到，不会。我想不出还有哪几对能复制它。不过，格雷格和我——至少从我的角度看，我相信格雷格会自己说——我们认识彼此已经非常久了。我对格雷格的能力有极大的尊重，无论是专业层面还是个人层面。我们之间的互动不像沃伦和查理那么频繁。但每个季度，我们都会通电话谈各自负责的业务，互相通报进展。然后在季度中间，我们也许没有那种正式的会议，但一旦出现与保险有关的问题，格雷格就会拿起电话打给我。同样，如果出现与格雷格负责的非保险业务有关的问题——比如最近我有一个客户在找买家，我就拿起电话打给格雷格，我们讨论怎么推进最好。所以，这种情况是有的。

每个季度我们也会交换笔记。我们两个人之间的关系运转得非常顺畅。我希望一直这样。格雷格？

**Greg Abel**（01:21:41）：是的。阿吉特，说得好。就像他提到的，沃伦和查理的关系是极其特殊的。但我为阿吉特和我之间的关系感到非常自豪。正如阿吉特所说，这是多年发展起来的。我们有机会——或者说我有机会——看到阿吉特是如何经营保险业务的。正如沃伦和查理强调的，没有人比他做得更好。所以我有机会观察到这一点。而多年来，这段关系也不断积累，越来越深厚。正如阿吉特提到的，我对他的人格和专业能力都怀有最高的敬意。虽然我们的互动方式可能和沃伦、查理不一样，但正如阿吉特所说，我们之间有定期的对话，既谈各自业务和子公司里的机会，也谈——如果我们看到什么不寻常的事、觉得对方应该知道，就一定会互相跟进。但这还不止于此。阿吉特非常理解伯克希尔的文化。

我坚信我也理解。每当我们的某项业务出现什么不寻常的情况，我会打给阿吉特，问：你觉得我们这样做稳妥吗？这是否符合你的想法，符合你在保险业务上的思路？所以，它不止是讨论业务，还在于维护并延续伯克希尔这种非凡的文化。所以，能有阿吉特这样的同事，我非常幸运，也非常享受每天和他共事。谢谢。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:19:44）：This question is from Jason Plawner. And he says, "Mr. Jain and Mr. Abel, this question is for you. "One of the successful features of Berkshire is the strong bond between Mr. Buffett and Mr. Munger, who manage the company better because they had each other. "As you two are clear leaders of the next generation at Berkshire Hathaway, can you please tell us about how you interact with each other, or some of the other incredibly competent Berkshire managers you seek for advice?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:11）：Who's that directed at?

**Becky Quick**（01:20:13）：Greg and Ajit —

**Charlie Munger**（01:20:14）：Ajit.

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:15）：OK.

**Ajit Jain**（01:20:16）：Well, there's no question that the relationship Warren has with Charlie is unique, and it's not going to be duplicated, certainly not by me and Greg, no. I can't think of very many other pairs that can duplicate it. Nevertheless, both Greg and I, at least certainly from my perspective, and I'm sure Greg will speak for himself, we've known each other for a very long time. I certainly have a lot of respect, both at a professional level and a personal level, in terms of what Greg's abilities are. We do not interact with each other as often as Warren and Charlie do. But every quarter, we will talk to each other about our respective businesses and update each other on our respective businesses. And then, during the course of the quarter, while we may not have any formal sort of meetings, if you will, but every time a question comes up which is related to insurance, Greg will pick up the phone and call me. By the same token, if there's any question that comes up relating to any of the non-insurance operations that Greg is in charge of, like we had recently where a client of mine was looking for — trying to find a buyer. And I picked up the phone and talked to Greg, and we talked about, you know, how best to proceed. So, that happens. And during the course of the quarter, every quarter we exchange notes. And we have a perfectly well-functioning relationship between the two of us. And I hope it remains that way. Greg?

**Greg Abel**（01:21:41）：Yes. Ajit, well said. And as he touched on, Warren and Charlie have an exceptional relationship. But I'm very proud of the relationship Ajit and I've had. And as Ajit touched on, it's developed over many years. We've had the opportunity — or I've had the opportunity — to see how Ajit's run the insurance business. And as Warren highlight and Charlie highlights, there's no one better at it. So, I've had the opportunity to observe that. And then, equally over the years, that relationship has just built and become greater and greater. And as Ajit touched on, couldn't have more personal respect for Ajit, both personally and professionally. And even though the interaction may be different than, say, how Warren and Charlie do it, as Ajit touched on, there is a regular dialogue, both around opportunities within our two businesses and units, both if we see something unusual that the other individual should hear, we make sure we're always following up with each other. But it goes beyond that. Ajit has a great understanding of the Berkshire culture. I strongly believe I do, too. And any time we see anything unusual in one of our businesses, it's Ajit who I'm going to call and say, are you comfortable that we're taking this approach? Is it going to be consistent with how you think about it, how you think about it in insurance? So, it goes — it goes beyond just discussing the businesses, but that maintaining the exceptional culture we have at Berkshire and building upon that. So, very fortunate to have Ajit as a colleague, and immensely enjoy working with him every day. Thank you.
