## 问题

biBERK 等网站在做小型商业险直销。策略是什么？能把这个市场「GEICO 化」吗？

## 回答要点

1. GEICO 1936 年就开始个人车险直销——「任何能降低成本、方便客户的模式最终都会成功」
2. 在小型商业险、工伤补偿等领域试验直销，支持有进取心的经理人
3. 「有些会失败，少数成功就会有非常好的业务」
4. 车险直销也不容易：州农→USAA→GEICO→前进保险一步步走来
5. 芒格：「可能会发生，但会很慢。」

## 关键引述

> **“we are experimenting, and we'll continue to experiment”**
>
> 「我们在试验，会继续试验」

**Warren Buffett**（02:12:43）：好，加里。

**Gary Ransom**（02:12:46）：我的问题是关于小型商业险的——特别是直销小型商业险。你们似乎有一些网站让买家直接购买小型商业保险，biBERK 是其中之一。这是竞争激烈、高度分散的市场。你们的策略是什么？最终能把小型商业险市场「GEICO 化」吗？

**Warren Buffett**（02:13:13）：我们会看到。这是个好问题，因为这正是我们问自己的。我们有 GEICO 这样了不起的公司，在个人车险直销上先行，1936 年就开始了。毫无疑问，经过很多年（也许不需要那么多年），小型商业险这类事情，任何能从体系中去除成本、让客户更方便的事情，只要你面对的是一个代理层层加价的旧体系，最终都会成功。我们在试验，会继续试验小型商业险、工伤补偿等。我们会想办法降低成本、以更低价格提供同等或更好的产品。我们不是唯一在做的。我们有一些会非常有进取心的经理人，我们支持他们。有些会失败，如果少数成功，我们就会有非常好的业务。世界正在往那个方向走。你可以期待我们跟着走。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:02）：嗯，如果容易的话，它会更快地——

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:08）：对——

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:09）：——发生。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:10）：它会在过程中发生的。想想车险当年也不容易。

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:15）：不，不容易。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:19）：对。那是一个带着各种多余成本的体系，可以追溯到 19 世纪末 20 世纪初。它建立在火灾保险和强大的总代理体系之上。1903 年福特造车后这些东西溢到了车险上。它在一个相对低效的体系中成长。州农（State Farm）率先走直销或专属代理路线。然后是 USAA，再后来是 GEICO，再后来是前进保险（Progressive），走向更高效、对消费者更友好的直销体系。类似的事会在各种行业中发生，小型商业险当然也是——

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:19）：可能会发生，但会很慢。

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:22）：需要长得惊人的时间。但——战斗不总是强者赢、比赛不总是快者胜。但按这个下注总是对的。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:12:43）：OK. Gary.

**Gary Ransom**（02:12:46）：My question is on small commercial, and specifically, direct small commercial. You seem to have some websites that enable buyers to purchase small commercial insurance directly; biBERK is one of them. It's a very competitive, fragmented market. But what is your strategy for that market? And then, can you ultimately GEICO-ize the small commercial market?

**Warren Buffett**（02:13:13）：Well, we'll find out. I mean, it's a very good question because that's exactly the question we ask ourselves. And we have this incredible company at GEICO, which has gone direct in the personal auto field, and was, you know, first started it in 1936. And there's no question in my mind that over a lot of years — and maybe not so many years — something like small commercial — anything that takes cost out of the system, you know, makes it easier for the customer, is going to work over time, if you've got a system that was based on something that had more layers of agency costs and that sort of thing. So we are experimenting, and we'll continue to experiment, on something like small commercial, workers' comp, whatever it may be. We'll try and figure out ways to take cost out of the system, offer the customer an equivalent product or better at lesser price, and we'll find out what can be done and what can't be done. And we're not the only ones doing it, as you know. But we are not going — we've got some managers that are going to be quite, I'm sure, enterprising on that. And we back them. And we expect some to fail and some — and if a few succeed — we'll have some very good businesses. And the world is going in that direction. So — you could expect us to try and go with it. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:02）：Well, if it were easy, I think it would've happened more fast —

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:08）：Yeah —

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:09）：— than it has.

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:10）：It will happen as we go along. I mean, it wasn't easy in auto, I mean, when you think about it.

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:15）：No, it wasn't.

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:19）：No. I mean, it was a system with all kinds of extra costs that go back to the turn of the 19th century into the 20th. I mean, it was built on fire insurance and strong general agencies. And that slopped over into auto when the auto came along in 1903 from Ford or whenever. And — so it grew within a system that really wasn't very efficient compared to what was available. But it took State Farm initially to go to a direct, or a captive agency system. And then it took USAA, and then later, GEICO, and then later, Progressive, to go to direct systems that are even more efficient and consumer-friendly. And the same thing is going to happen, to some degree, in all kinds of industries, and certainly small commercial — somebody will —

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:19）：It could happen, but it will be slow.

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:22）：It takes an amazingly long time. I mean, it — but you know, the battle doesn't always go to the strong and the race to the swift. But that's the way to bet, you know, as they say. So — (Laughs)
