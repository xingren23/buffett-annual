## 问题

与贝索斯和戴蒙合作挑战医疗行业遇到的困难，说明行业护城河比预想的宽。这是否意味着 PBM 等现有玩家理应享有更高市盈率？

## 回答要点

1. 体系对外来者有护城河，但体系内每个参与者不一定有各自的护城河
2. 「我们攻击的是行业层面的护城河」——定义为整个医疗，不只是保险公司
3. 目标是改善医疗质量，不是牺牲质量换成本
4. 芒格：民主党掌权后会走向单一支付——「对很多 PBM 不太友好。我不会想念它们。」

## 关键引述

> **“we are attacking an industry moat”**
>
> 「我们攻击的是行业层面的护城河」

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:37）：好，8 号站台？

**Audience Member**（02:16:45）：我是佛罗里达州杰克逊维尔的奥斯汀·梅里亚姆。巴菲特先生，最近您与贝索斯先生和戴蒙先生合作挑战医疗行业的新闻——加上你们自己承认遇到的困难——让我相信这个行业的进入壁垒比最初设想的更高，护城河更宽。这是否意味着该行业现有玩家（比如 PBM）理应享有更高的市盈率？

**Warren Buffett**（02:17:18）：嗯——虽然这个体系对外来者可能有护城河，但体系内的每个参与者不一定各自有护城河。如果这个新三巨头真能成事，我们攻击的是行业层面的护城河。我定义的「行业」很广——是整个医疗，不只是医疗保险公司。我们想找到更好的方式，同时确保不牺牲医疗质量。目标是改善医疗。

**Charlie Munger**（02:18:35）：嗯，我猜最终当民主党同时控制国会两院和白宫时，我们会走向全民单一支付的医疗。我认为那对很多现有的 PBM 不太友好。（掌声）我不会想念它们。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:37）：OK, station 8?

**Audience Member**（02:16:45）：Austin Merriam, from Jacksonville, Florida. Mr. Buffett, with the recent news of the partnership between you, Mr. [Jeff] Bezos, and Mr. [Jamie] Dimon, to challenge the health care industry and the self-admitted difficulties you are running across, this would lead me to believe the industry has higher barriers to entry than may have originally been hypothesized; a larger moat, if you will. Would that justify a higher earnings multiple for established players in the industries, such as PBMs, for example?

**Warren Buffett**（02:17:18）：Well, just — though the system may have a moat against intruders, it doesn't mean that everybody operating within the system has individual moats, for one thing. Now, I — we are — if this new triumvirate succeeds at all, we are attacking an industry moat. And I'm defining industry very broadly; health care, not just, you know, health care insurers or this or that. We're trying to figure out a better way of doing it and making sure that we're not sacrificing care. And the goal is to improve care. And like I say, that is a — that's a lot bigger than a single company's moat. It's bigger than a component of the industry's moat. The moat held by the whole system, since it interacts in so many ways, is actually — that's the moat that essentially has to be attacked, and that's a huge moat. And like I say, we'll do our best. But — I hope if we fail, I hope somebody else succeeds. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:18:35）：Well, I suspect that eventually when the Democrats control both houses of Congress and the White House, we will get single-payer medicine. And I don't think it's going to be very friendly to many of the current PBMs. (Applause) And I won't miss them. (Laughter)
