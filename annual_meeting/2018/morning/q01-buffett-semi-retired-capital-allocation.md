## 问题

股东问：阿贝尔和贾因已接管管理者监督，资本配置又与韦施勒和康姆斯分担，巴菲特是否已算半退休？

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特笑称自己「半退休几十年了」
2. 泰德·韦施勒和托德·康姆斯各管约 120—130 亿美元，合计约 250 亿；股票 1700 多亿、长期债券 200 亿、现金及短期 1000 亿仍由巴菲特负责
3. 阿吉特·贾因和格雷格·阿贝尔精力充沛，但伯克希尔的文化是经理人自治
4. 芒格：「没事可干的时候，沃伦非常擅长什么都不干」

## 关键引述

> **“I've been semi-retired for decades.”**
>
> 「我已经半退休几十年了。」

> **“Part of the Berkshire secret is that when there's nothing to do, Warren is very good at doing nothing.”**
>
> 「伯克希尔的秘密之一就是，没事可干的时候，沃伦非常擅长什么都不干。」

**Warren Buffett**（00:20:02）：好，开场白说完了，我们进入提问环节。先从卡罗尔开始。

**Carol Loomis**（00:20:11）：早上好。每年选第一个问题，我都会找一个肯定跟伯克希尔有关、又足够应景的问题。今年这个问题正合适。提问者是俄勒冈州塞勒姆的威廉·安德森（音）。他说：「巴菲特先生，您过去说过，您的工作有两部分——监督管理者和资本配置。阿贝尔先生和贾因先生现在负责监督管理者，剩下资本配置由您管。但资本配置您又跟韦施勒和康姆斯分担。这是不是意味着您已经是半退休状态了？如果不是，请解释一下。」（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（00:20:56）：我已经半退休几十年了。（笑）说实话，很难拆出一个比例来——以前由我做、现在归阿吉特和格雷格管的那些事，以及投资这块归泰德和托德负责的那部分。泰德和托德各管 120 到 130 亿美元，加起来 250 亿左右。我们股票持仓大概 1700 多亿，长期债券 200 亿，现金和短期投资还有 1000 亿。所以他们管着 250 亿，干得非常好。剩下的 3000 亿基本还是我负责。（笑·掌声）我想查理会告诉你——其实我希望他来说说——一切真的没什么大变化。阿吉特和格雷格确实更聪明、更有精力，每天都能带来更多的东西。但他们也不会带来太多变化，因为我们的文化就是各子公司的经理自己管自己的业务。不过需要盯的事情确实不少，所以他们干得非常出色。泰德和托德不仅把各自那 120、130 亿管得很好——他们接手时每人只有几十亿——也不光是那笔钱翻了几倍的问题——他们还为伯克希尔做了很多事，做得很乐意，更重要的是做得很漂亮。我不断有事情交给他们去查、去做。有时候我还偷他们的主意。（笑）但我觉得，「半退休」这个词其实把我描述得太清闲了。提问者说得有道理。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（00:23:30）：嗯，我观察沃伦很久了，他大部分时间坐在那里读书、思考，偶尔打个电话或跟人聊聊。我看不出有什么大区别。很多人——（笑）伯克希尔的秘密之一就是，没事可干的时候，沃伦非常擅长什么都不干。（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（00:23:53）：我还等着当床垫测试员呢。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（00:20:02）：So with all those happy words, we will move on and start the questioning, and we'll start with Carol.

**Carol Loomis**（00:20:11）：Good morning. In choosing a first question to ask each year, I look for a question that is definitely Berkshire-related and is timely. And this question seemed to fill the bill. The question came from William Anderson (PH) of Salem, Oregon. And he said, "Mr. Buffett, you have previously said that there are two parts to your job, overseeing the managers and capital allocation. Mr. [Greg] Abel and Mr. [Ajit] Jain now oversee the managers, which leaves you with capital allocation. "However, you share capital allocation with Ted Weschler and Todd Combs. Question. Does all that mean you are semi-retired? Or if not, please explain." (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（00:20:56）：I've been semi-retired for decades. (Laughter) The answer is that I was probably — well, it's hard to break down the percentage of the time that I was involved in but now — the jobs that are now done by Ajit and Greg, and in the case of investing, the sub part of the job that is done by Ted and Todd. Ted and Todd each manage 12- or $13 billion, so in total, that's 25 billion. And we have in equities 170-some billion, probably now, and 20 billion in longer-term bonds, and another hundred billion in cash and short-term. So they're managing 20 - 25 and doing a very good job. And I still have the responsibility, basically, for the other 300 billion. So — (Laughter and applause) I think Charlie will tell you — in fact, I'd like him to comment — nothing's really changed that much. We’ve got — clearly we've got two people in Ajit and Greg that are smarter, more energetic, just bring more to the job every day. But they don't bring too much, because the culture is that our managers are running their business. But there's a lot — there’s a good bit to oversee. So they do a superb job. And Ted and Todd not only do a great job with the 12 or 13 billion each — they started with a couple billion each — not that it’s all been the growth of the 2 billion — but they also do — have done a number of things for Berkshire that they do it cheerfully, but more important, very skillfully. So there's just — there's one thing after another that I will have them looking into or working on. And sometimes I steal their ideas and — But I think, actually, semi-retired is probably — catches me at my most active point. I think if —(laughter) — your questioner’s got a good point. OK, Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（00:23:30）：Well, I've watched Warren for a long time, and he sits around reading most of the time and thinking. And every once in a while he talks on the phone or talks to somebody. I can't see any great difference. A lot of people — (Laughter) Part of the Berkshire secret is that when there's nothing to do, Warren is very good at doing nothing. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（00:23:53）：I'm still looking forward to being a mattress tester. (Laughter)
