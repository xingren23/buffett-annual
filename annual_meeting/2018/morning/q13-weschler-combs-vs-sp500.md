## 问题

何时公布泰德和托德管理的 250 亿的年度业绩？过去五年有没有跑赢标普 500？

## 回答要点

1. 「大概永远不会单独报告他们的个人业绩」
2. 两人从各自起点以来的记录「几乎一模一样」——都与标普持平
3. 托德在医疗项目上贡献巨大，泰德做的几件事「比我自己还好」
4. 芒格打趣：「你去年报告了，今年没报。现在报告了。」
5. 规模是核心难题：管 120 亿比管 10 亿更难

## 关键引述

> **“the record, since inception, is almost identical”**
>
> 「从各自起点以来的记录几乎一模一样」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:27:15）：好，贝基？

**Becky Quick**（01:27:18）：这个问题来自 Wedgewood Partners 的大卫·罗尔夫，他的公司自 1989 年起就是伯克希尔股东。伯克希尔目前是他们 18 只股票持仓中最大的一笔。他问：「过去两年你们列出了 Protégé Partners 的基金中基金业绩。什么时候开始公布泰德和托德管理的 250 亿美元的年度业绩？过去五年里泰德或托德有没有跑赢标普 500？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:27:51）：嗯。第一，我们大概永远不会单独报告他们的个人业绩。但你可以放心，我和查理都极其关注他们为伯克希尔贡献了多少。他们非常出色。他们不仅有智慧和业绩记录，还是非常优秀的人。托德在医疗项目上做了大量工作。泰德——我交给他几件事，他做得比我自己还好。从各自起点算——泰德比托德晚大约一年——两个人从各自起点以来的记录几乎一模一样——都与标普持平。他们也拿到了一些激励报酬，只有在跑赢标普时才能拿到。如我所说，他们只是略微领先。这真的……比我做得更好，所以我自然没法批评。（笑）他们是两个非常、非常、非常好的选择。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:29:13）：你去年报告了。只是今年没报。（笑）现在报告了。（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:29:48）：问题是规模。说实话，管 120 亿、130 亿比管 10 亿更难。如果只管 100 万，那完全是另一回事。你同意吧，查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:07）：当然。

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:08）：好。（笑）就像好律师一样——除非你知道答案，否则别问。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:27:15）：OK. Becky?

**Becky Quick**（01:27:18）：This question comes from David Rolfe, who is with Wedgewood Partners, and has been — the company — has been shareholders in Berkshire since 1989. The stock is currently the largest holding in their stocks — 18 stocks. He asks this question: "Over the past two years, you have listed the individual fund-of-funds performance from Protégé Partners. When will you start showing the annual performance on 25 billion that Ted [Weschler] and Todd [Combs] manage? Can you state if either Ted or Todd has beaten the S&P 500 index over the last five years?

**Warren Buffett**（01:27:51）：Yeah. Both — A, we'll probably never report their individual performance. But you can be sure that I have an enormous interest in — as does Charlie — in how much we think they contribute to Berkshire. And they have — they've been terrific. They've — they not only have the intellect, and the record, but they are exceptional human beings. And they — Todd has done a tremendous amount of work, for example, on the medical project. And — Ted is — I've given him several things, and he's done them better than I could do them. So the record, since inception — and I'm measuring it — Ted came later than Todd, a year or so later — but the record, since inception, is almost identical — both for the two managers — from their different inception and matching the S&P. And they've received some incentive compensation, which they only get if they beat the S&P. And as I say, they're just slightly ahead. That really hasn't — It's been better than I've done, so naturally, I can't criticize it. (Laughs) They — they were the — they were two very, very, very good choices. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:29:13）：You did report it in a previous year. You just didn't do it this year. And — but now you have your report. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（01:29:48）：I would — the problem that all of us has is size. It's actually — it's harder to run even 12 or $13 billion, frankly, than it is to run a billion. And if you're running a million dollars or something of the sort, it's a whole different game. You'd agree with that, wouldn't you, Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:30:07）：Of course.

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:08）：Yeah, OK. (Laughter) Just like any good lawyer, you never ask him a question unless you think you know the answer they're going to give. (Laughter)
