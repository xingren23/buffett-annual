## 问题

如果投资组合只有 10 亿美元，会怎样调整投资？会更多考虑中国等新兴市场吗？

## 回答要点

1. 10 亿在美国 30 万亿市场能找到比大资金更好的机会
2. 巴菲特 15 年前曾翻韩国股票名录买过 20 家统计上便宜的公司
3. 「绝不会排除新兴市场」；小资金能做大资金做不了的事
4. 芒格：「我在中国的股票比你多——按比例算」
5. 更年轻的大市场会有奇怪的定价错位——「市场随成熟走向效率」

## 关键引述

> **“there's no way I'd rule out emerging markets”**
>
> 「我绝不会排除新兴市场」

> **“I already have more stocks in China than you do, as a percentage”**
>
> 「我在中国的股票比你多——按比例算」

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:50:20）：好，6 号站台？

**Audience Member**（01:50:21）：你好，早上好，巴菲特先生和芒格先生。我是余思迪（音），来自上海的 Horizon Insights——一家专注中国的研究公司。我有很多中国的公募基金客户，他们很年轻——相对年轻——管理的资金规模较小。我的问题是，如果今天您的投资组合只有 10 亿美元，您会怎样调整投资？会更多考虑中国等新兴市场的投资机会吗？谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（01:51:11）：如果只有 10 亿，我可能会在美国这个 30 万亿美元的市场里找到机会——我对那里的了解总体上比对世界其他地方深——而且找到的机会可能会比我们为几千亿资金找的好不少。但我不会——绝不可能排除新兴市场。大约 15 年前，纯粹因为有趣、也让我回忆起年轻时光，我周末翻了一本韩国股票名录。我买了——都是小股票——按韩国或美国的标准其实不算小，是很大的公司。但我找到了 15 到 20 家统计上便宜的，每家买了一点。小资金能做的事我们大资金做不了。但我第一反应总是先翻美国的东西。但我也翻过其他国家。我大概不会进非常非常小的市场，因为执行和税务上可能有很多麻烦……

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:02）：嗯，我在中国的股票比你多——按比例算——所以我站在这位女士这边。（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:19）：好。你想报下股票名字吗？这些股票有名字吗？（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:20）：不，不说。（巴菲特笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:25）：卡罗尔？

**Carol Loomis**（01:53:28）：这个问题是——

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:29）：我补充一点。你们会在中国找到很多机会。查理会说，即使资金相同，中国的猎场比美国还好。你同意吗？

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:40）：对，我同意。

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:45）：对。从逻辑上讲应该是这样——因为那是一个更年轻但同样庞大的市场。市场大概会随着成熟走向效率。30 年前日本的认股权证定价非常奇怪。人们过一阵就发现了，然后就消失了。但市场在发展过程中会出现一些非常奇怪的事。你同意吧，查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:54:23）：绝对同意。

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:24）：对。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:50:20）：OK. Station 6?

**Audience Member**（01:50:21）：Hi, good morning, Mr. Buffett and Mr. Munger. My name is Stephie Yu from Horizon Insights, a China-focused research firm based in Shanghai. So I have a lot of mutual fund clients in China, who are very young — relatively younger — and they manage a smaller portion of funds. So my question is, if you only have $1 billion in your portfolio today, how would you change your investments? Would you consider more investment opportunities in emerging markets such as China? Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（01:51:11）：Yeah. I would say, if I were working with a billion, I would probably find — within a $30 trillion market in the United States, where I understood things better, generally, than I do around the world — I'd probably find opportunities there that would be better, incidentally, by some margin, than what we can find for hundreds of billions. But I wouldn't — there's no way I'd rule out emerging markets. There was a time, 15 years ago or so, when just because it was kind of interesting and it took me back to my youth, I — on the weekend, I went through a directory of Korean stocks. And I bought — and these were small stocks — well, they weren't small by standards of either Korean or American business. They were big, big companies. But I found 15 or 20 in — that were statistically cheap and bought some of each one myself. And there are opportunities with smaller amounts of money to do things that we just can't do. And — but I — my first inclination always would be to comb through things in the United States. And — But I've combed through — in other countries. I probably wouldn't get into very, very small markets because there can be a lot of difficulties even in market execution and taxation, (inaudible). You can find — if you can't find it, you know, in America and China and Britain and a few other places — (laughs) — you probably aren't going to find it someplace else. You may think you've found it. But that may be — it may be a different game than you know. Our problem is size, not geography. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:02）：Well, I already have more stocks in China than you do, as a percentage, so I'm with the young lady. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:19）：OK. Well, you can — you want to name names? Do these stocks have names? Or — (Laughs)

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:20）：No, I don't. (Buffett laughs)

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:25）：Carol?

**Carol Loomis**（01:53:28）：This question is —

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:29）：I should just add one thing. You will find plenty of opportunities in China. Charlie would say you've got a better hunting ground than even a person with similar capital in the United States. Would you agree with —?

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:40）：Yes, I do.

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:45）：Yeah, yeah. So — and in the sense they're — it's logical that should be the case because it's a younger market, but still a large market. So that — Markets probably work toward efficiency as they age. Japan had this very strange situation with warrants being priced out of line and all of that 30 years ago. And people notice after a while and it disappears. But there can be — some very strange things happen in markets as they develop. I think you'd agree with that, Charlie, wouldn't you?

**Charlie Munger**（01:54:23）：Absolutely.

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:24）：Yeah.
