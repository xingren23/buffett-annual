## 问题

GEICO 去年实现了增长但综合成本率 15 年来首次破百。剔除巨灾后损失趋势有什么变化？为何放慢增速？

## 回答要点

1. 并非主动放慢——「我从来就没想让 GEICO 放慢增长」
2. 若无巨灾承保本可略有盈余；「剔除某某」在保险里没意义
3. 一季度利润率约 7%，四月数据持平——承保利润率完全令人满意
4. 市场份额从 1993 年的 2% 出头增至全美家庭的 13%，「会继续增加」
5. 为客户每年省 40—50 亿美元；去年贡献 20 亿浮存金
6. 芒格：「从来就没差过，现在更好了。」

## 关键引述

> **“GEICO is a jewel.”**
>
> 「GEICO 是一颗明珠。」

> **“It was never very bad, and it's better now.”**
>
> 「从来就没差过，现在更好了。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:19）：好，加里？

**Gary Ransom**（01:30:23）：我的问题是关于 GEICO 的。去年你们承诺增长、也实现了。但过程中综合成本率在上升，大约 15 年来首次超过 100。当然有一部分是巨灾。但即使剔除巨灾，损失趋势里也有一些情况导致你们放慢了增长——至少在年底前后。能告诉我们发生了什么吗？我今早也看了一下，一季度似乎稳定了一些，但我还是想知道四季度的情况。

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:05）：当然。我跟你的问题只有一点不同：你说它导致我们放慢，但我们并不想放慢增速。我从来就没想让 GEICO 放慢增长。增速确实放缓了，但不是因为我们想。导致承保亏损的定价，如果没有巨灾，我们其实会略有盈余。但如果不付电费也会有盈余。（笑）这种「剔除某某」在保险里没什么意义。

看一季度，我们的利润率约 7%，实际上略高于目标。我拿到了四月的初步数据，也差不多。承保利润，也就是利润率，现在完全令人满意。我们希望获得尽可能多的增长。今年我们会增加市场份额。1993 年托尼·尼切利接手时是 2% 出头，现在是全美家庭的 13%。我们会继续增加份额，继续盈利承保，大部分时候。偶尔费率会稍微不准（不足），或者遇到飓风大损失、纽约的桑迪飓风之类。

GEICO 是一颗明珠。我们还有几家同样珍视的公司，但它了不起。它有自己的文化。按汽车保险平均价格算，它每年为客户省下大约 40 到 50 亿美元。它大部分年份承保都有利润。去年又贡献了 20 亿美元浮存金。它是一家很棒的公司。如我所说，前四个月明显好转。汽车保险有些季节性，一季度通常是四个季度中最好的。但季节性不是特别强。所以看 10-Q（四月数据你们可以信我），GEICO 在利润和增长上都在正轨。增长越多，我越高兴。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:34:25）：嗯，你说得很完美。

**Warren Buffett**（01:34:30）：嗯？

**Charlie Munger**（01:34:31）：从来就没差过，现在更好了。（巴菲特笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:30:19）：OK. Gary?

**Gary Ransom**（01:30:23）：My question's on GEICO. Last year, you promised growth and delivered. But along the way, the combined ratio was moving up, and it was the first time it was over a hundred in about 15 years. Granted, some of that was catastrophes. But even excluding catastrophes, there was something going on in the loss trends that caused you to slow down that growth, at least at the — as we got to the latter part of the year. And I wondered if you could tell us what was going on. And I did look this morning, too, so it looked like the first quarter has settled down a little bit, but I'd still like to know about the fourth quarter.

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:05）：Yeah, sure. It — the only thing I differ with the question on slightly — when you say it caused us to slow down — we didn't want to slow down the growth. I mean, you're looking at a guy here that has never wanted to slow down the growth of GEICO. The growth did slow down, but it wasn't because we wanted it to. Our prices that led to the underwriting loss — we actually — we'd have been slightly in the black without the catastrophes. But, you know, if we hadn't have paid our light bills, we might have been in the black, too. I mean, this "except for" stuff doesn't mean much in insurance as far as I'm concerned. The — if you'll look at the first quarter — our margins were around 7 percent, which is actually a little more than we aimed for. And I received the unaudited — I mean, the preliminary — figures for April, and they're similar. So, the underwriting gain is — or margins — are perfectly satisfactory now. And we'd love to get all the growth we can. And we will gain market share this year. And we gained market share — Tony — when Tony [Nicely] took over the place, it was — in 1993 — it was two and a very small fraction percent. And it'll be 13 percent of the — you know — 13 percent of the households in the country now. And we will keep gaining share. We will keep writing profitably — most of the time. And every now and then, our rates will be slightly — modestly inaccurate — inadequate, I should say. And/or we'll have, maybe, some big losses on hurricanes or something of the sort, or we'll have a [Hurricane] Sandy in New York. The — but GEICO is a jewel. And it's — you know, it's really a — we've got some others we feel awfully close to similarly about, but it's an incredible company. It has a culture all of its own. It's saving its customers probably 4 or $5 billion a year against which they would — against what they would otherwise be paying, based on the average in auto insurance. And it will be profitable on underwriting a very high percentage of the year. It contributed another $2 billion to float last year. It is a terrific company. And like I say, the first four months are dramatically better. Now, there's some seasonal in auto insurance. So, the first quarter is usually the best of the four quarters. But it's not a dramatic seasonal. So, I think when you read the 10-Q — and you can take my word for April — I think GEICO is on a good profit track as well as a good growth track. And the more it grows, the better I like it. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:34:25）：Well, I think you've said it perfectly.

**Warren Buffett**（01:34:30）：Huh.

**Charlie Munger**（01:34:31）：It was never very bad, and it's better now. (Buffett laughs)
