## 问题

伯克希尔从纺织小厂变成了《财富》500 强第四。未来 50 年呢？2068 年的伯克希尔是什么样子？

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特：「我不知道。50 年前我也不知道现在会是什么样。」
2. 会基于某些原则；「非常希望和相信会像任何大公司一样以股东为导向」
3. 芒格对年轻股东：「我们走了以后卖掉伯克希尔股票的人会更糟——保持信念。」
4. 巴菲特笑：「下次我来给他的答案，既然它拿了这么多掌声。」

## 关键引述

> **“I would advise you to keep the faith.”**
>
> 「我建议你们保持信念。」

**Carol Loomis**（01:54:36）：这个问题——我承认不是个小问题——来自蒙特利尔的吉迪恩·波拉克。他说：「全世界大致知道伯克希尔·哈撒韦自 1965 年以来的样子变了多少。那时它是一家小小的东北部纺织公司，如今是《财富》500 强第四。未来 50 年呢？能否告诉我们 2068 年的伯克希尔是什么样子？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:55:15）：我想它会看起来遥不可及。（笑）不，答案是我不知道。50 年前我也不知道现在会是什么样。它会基于某些原则。但会走到哪里——我们会看到，会有跟我想法不同的人，世界也会不同。但我非常希望并相信，我们会像世界上任何一家大公司一样以股东为导向。我们会把股东当合伙人，用他们的钱就像用自己的钱一样，不会想着占便宜。谁知道到时还会发生什么？查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:56:16）：嗯，我想跟在座的年轻股东说几句。我们走了以后，你们中那些卖掉伯克希尔股票、在众多朋友帮助下拿去做别的事的人，我猜会更糟。（笑）所以我建议你们保持信念。（掌声）顺便说一下，很多家庭已经发生过这种事了。

**Warren Buffett**（01:56:49）：下次我来给他的答案，既然看到它拿到这么多掌声。（笑）

He says, "The world knows generally how the looks of Berkshire Hathaway have changed since you began to run the company in 1965. Berkshire was then a tiny northeastern, textile company. And now it is the number-four company on the Fortune 500. "What about the next 50 years? Could you give us your view of what Berkshire looks like in 2068?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:55:15）：I think it'll look a long way away. (Laughter) No, the answer is I don't know. And I didn't know, 50 years ago, what it would like now, I mean — It will be based on certain principles. But where that leads, you know, we will find out and we'll have people that are thinking about different things than I am. And we'll have a world that's different. But — We will be — I very much hope and believe that we will be — that we'll be as shareholder-oriented as any large company in the world. We will look at our shareholders as partners and we will be trying to do with their money exactly what we'd do with our own, not seeking to get an edge on them. And who knows what else will be happening then? Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:56:16）：Well, I want to talk to the younger shareholders in the group. Those of you who, after we are gone, sell your Berkshire stock and do something else with it, helped by your many friends, I think are going to do worse. (Laughter) So I would advise you to keep the faith. (Applause) By the way, some of that has already happened in many families.

**Warren Buffett**（01:56:49）：I'll give his answer next time now that I see it get all of that applause. (Laughter)
