## 问题

股东质疑：巴菲特说企业不应在枪支政策中扮演角色、伯克希尔持枪支制造商股票也没问题——这与所罗门听证会展现的道德罗盘不符？

## 回答要点

1. 「应该只是我的观点，还是由公司所有者来决定？」
2. 母公司层面从未做过政治献金；以个人身份表态不代表伯克希尔
3. GEICO 投保单上不该有「你是 NRA 成员吗」之类的问题
4. 「哪些公司是纯洁的」非常难判断
5. 芒格：「在奥马哈被野火鸡包围着，我们当然不会禁掉所有枪。」

## 关键引述

> **“I do not believe in imposing my political opinions on the activities of our businesses.”**
>
> 「我不信把我的政治观点强加于业务活动。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:08）：安德鲁？

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（01:41:09）：好，沃伦。这个问题来自一位持股十年的伯克希尔股东。我得说这可能是我收到的最尖锐的问题之一。所以——

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:19）：但你还是决定拿出来。（笑）

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（01:41:20）：确实。（笑）股东写道：「每年年会我都看那段视频——您代表所罗门在国会作证，那是道德罗盘的象征。投资者越来越看重有社会和道德责任感的企业。所以当 CNBC 问您企业在枪支销售的合理政策中能扮演什么角色时，您说企业根本不应该有角色、您不会把价值观强加于人——我感到不安。当您说伯克希尔持有枪支制造商的股票也没问题时，我更惊讶。多年前的年会上您说过不会买烟草公司，因为社会问题。伯克希尔只要合法就跟任何公司扯上关系的想法，与我认为您所代表的一切格格不入。请告诉我们您说错了。」

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:20）：嗯——（掌声）——我们来探讨一下。（笑）应该只是我的观点，还是应该由公司所有者来决定？如果我决定就各种政治议题征求股东意见——比如伯克希尔是否应该支持 NRA——如果多数股东投票支持、或多数董事投票支持，我会接受。我不认为我在接任这份工作时把政治观点放进了盲信。2016 年大选我筹了不少钱——我筹的钱给了希拉里。我也以相当坦率的方式表过态——（掌声）——但那时我不是在替伯克希尔说话。我是以一个普通公民的身份。我不认为我有权替伯克希尔发言。母公司层面我们从未做过政治献金。我也不去找供应商筹款——无论是给我上过的学校还是政治候选人。我也不认为 GEICO 的投保单上该有「您是 NRA 成员吗？」这样的问题——如果是，你就配不上我们之类的。我不信把我的政治观点强加于业务活动。至于哪些公司是「纯洁」的——（掌声）——我认为非常难判断。谢谢。有这个回答之后，我都有点不敢叫查理了。但请吧，查理。（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:30）：嗯，沃伦，显然你还是有底线的——

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:32）：对，我们有。

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:33）：在各种事情上——

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:34）：对。

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:37）：——有些事即使合法也是我们不做的。但我们不一定是靠什么至高知识划的线。我们只是尽力而为。而且，在奥马哈被野火鸡包围着，我们当然不会禁掉所有枪。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:08）：Andrew?

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（01:41:09）：OK, Warren. This question comes from a Berkshire shareholder who says they've been a shareholder for ten years. I should say this may be one of the most pointed questions I've ever received for you. So —

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:19）：But you elected to give it, though.

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（01:41:20）：But I did. (Laughter) The shareholder writes, "I have watched the movie every year at this meeting, when you testify in front of Congress on behalf of Salomon, as the symbol of what it means to have a moral compass. Investors are increasingly looking to invest in companies that are socially and morally responsible. "So I was disturbed when you were asked on CNBC about the role that business could play in sensible policies around the sales of guns. "You said you didn't think business should have a role at all, and you wouldn't impose your values on others. I was even more surprised when you said you'd be OK with Berkshire owning shares in gun manufacturers. "At this meeting years ago, you said you wouldn't buy a tobacco company because of the social issues. The idea that Berkshire would associate with any company as long as it isn't illegal seems at odds with everything I think you stand for. Please tell us you misspoke."

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:20）：Well — (applause) — let's explore that a little. (Laughter) Should it be just my view, or should it be the view of the owners of the company? So, if I decide to poll the owners of the company on a variety of political issues, and one of them being whether, you know, Berkshire Hathaway should support the NRA, I don't — if a majority of the shareholders voted to do it, or if a majority of the board of directors voted to do it, I would — I wouldn't — I would accept that. I don't think that the — my political views — I don't think I put them in a blind trust at all when I take the job. And I — in the elections of 2016, I raised a lot of money. In my case, I raised it for Hillary [Clinton]. And I spoke out in various ways that were quite frank, but — (applause) — I don't think that I speak — When I do that, I don't think I'm speaking for Berkshire. I'm speaking as a private citizen. And I don't think I have any business speaking for Berkshire. We have never — at the parent company level — we have never made a political contribution, you know — And I don't go to our suppliers. I don't do anything of that sort where I raise money either for the school I went to, or for a political candidate I went to, or anything else. And I don't think that we should have a question on the GEICO policyholder form, "Are you an NRA member?" you know, and if you are, you just aren't good enough for us, or something. That — I think — I do not believe in imposing my political opinions on the activities of our businesses. And if you get to what companies are pure and which ones aren't pure — (applause) — I think it is very difficult to make that call. Thank you. I think with that response, I'm almost afraid to call on Charlie. But go ahead, Charlie. (Laughter)

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:30）：Well, obviously, you do draw a limit, Warren —

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:32）：Yeah, we did.

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:33）：— in all kinds of thing —

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:34）：Yeah.

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:37）：— which are beneath us, even though they're legal. But we don't necessarily draw it perfectly because we've got some sort of supreme knowledge. We just do the best we can. And certainly, we're not going to ban all guns, surrounded by wild turkeys in Omaha. (Laughter)
