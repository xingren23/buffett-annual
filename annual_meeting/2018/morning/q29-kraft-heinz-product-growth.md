## 问题

消费者口味在变、卡夫亨氏利润率已经很高。不靠收购，现有品牌加新品能维持或提高利润吗？除了番茄酱还有哪些产品在增长？

## 回答要点

1. 「你其实是在问我卡夫亨氏是不是好股票」——不便透露
2. 除了番茄酱确实有不少产品需求在增长，因地区差异很大
3. 「消费品包装行业就投入资产回报而言仍是极好的生意」
4. 全球人口增长缓慢，但消费者更愿意尝试新产品和有机产品
5. 新产品不断涌现

## 关键引述

> **“Consumer packaged goods are still a terrific business in terms of return on invested assets.”**
>
> 「就投入资产回报而言，消费品包装行业仍是极好的生意。」

**Warren Buffett**（02:37:37）：好，休息前再问一个。乔纳森？

**Jonathan Brandt**（02:37:41）：考虑到食品行业消费者口味的变化和卡夫亨氏已经很高的利润率结构，您认为他们现有的品牌加上新品推出，能否在未来十年不靠收购就维持或提高当前利润水平？除了番茄酱，还有哪些产品需求在增长？

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:09）：嗯，你其实是在问我卡夫亨氏是不是好股票。我们不想以这种方式透露可交易证券的信息。但——是的，除了番茄酱还有不少产品需求在增长。有些因地区差异很大。不同产品的渗透率差别巨大。就投入资产回报而言，消费品包装行业仍是极好的生意。但全球人口增长相当缓慢。人们吃的量差不多。人们更愿意尝试有机产品之类的。这是很好的生意。新产品不断涌现……

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:37:37）：OK, we'll have one more question before we break. Jonathan?

**Jonathan Brandt**（02:37:41）：Given the changes in consumer tastes in the food business, and Kraft Heinz's already high margin structure, do you think the brands they own today, plus new product introductions, can together maintain or increase the current level of profits over the next ten years without the benefit of acquisitions? Is there anything in their portfolio besides ketchup that is enjoying growing demand?

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:09）：Well, in effect, you're asking me whether Kraft Heinz is a good buy. And we don't — (laughs) — we don't want to give information on marketable securities in that manner. But — yeah, there are a number of items besides ketchup that enjoy growing demand. And some vary quite a bit by geography. There's enormous differences in the penetration of various products in the portfolio. Consumer packaged goods are still a terrific business in terms of return on invested assets. And you know — but the population, worldwide, grows fairly smally and at — a fairly minor rate. And — people are going to eat about the same amount. And there is some more willingness to experiment, you know, or go for organic products of the sort. It's a very good business. And there are new products coming out constantly. It's not one where you're going to get terrific organic growth, but it never has been. And — you know, I like the business and we own 26 or so percent of it. But there are a number of items within Kraft Heinz that enjoy pretty — fairly — healthy growth. And I think you'd find that at most food companies. And I think you'd find very good returns on invested — on tangible net assets — at those businesses.
