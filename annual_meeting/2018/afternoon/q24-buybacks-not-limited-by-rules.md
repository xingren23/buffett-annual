## 问题

保险子公司分红受法规限制（2018 年最多 160 亿）。这是否也是回购的上限？

## 回答要点

1. 遵守州法规；保险子公司确实限制年度股利
2. 回购不受此限——「如果回购真的很有吸引力，我们会做得非常大」
3. 芒格：「可以获得特别许可宣派更多股利——不该认为被自然法则限制」

## 关键引述

> **“if repurchases were really attractive, we would do it in a very big way”**
>
> 「如果回购真的很有吸引力，我们会做得非常大」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

**Warren Buffett**（01:58:51）：好。格雷格？

**Gregg Warren**（01:58:53）：沃伦，今年年报里像往常一样提到：保险子公司派发股息受保险法规和其他监管限制，伯克希尔保险业务 2018 年目前最多可宣布 160 亿美元的常规股息。

我的问题是：这个年度监管上限，是否也应该被看作股票回购的可用额度基准？如果伯克希尔想回购更多股票、或者派发更多股息，你动用不归保险业务持有的经营资本来回馈股东，会不会有问题？

附带一问：由国民赔偿公司账上持有的 BNSF 每年上缴的现金分配，会不会被排除在外？

**Warren Buffett**（01:59:50）：是的。我们显然会遵守注册地的州法规，当然也遵守所有规则。基本是保险公司注册所在州的规定，它们确实限制任何一年的股息金额，虽然你如果愿意，可以申请额外额度。但我们从没考虑过那样做。

至于回购：如果回购真的很有吸引力，我们会大规模地做。我不排除这种可能。我们可以用各种方式安排，去做一笔非常大的收购（这是我更偏好的），或者一笔非常大的回购。后者我认为大概不太会发生，原因是我们股票的成交方式，而不是我们不愿意在大幅折价时买。

所以查理和我有胃口。我们有很多现金，而且还可以有更多现金。任何规模、哪怕是极大的一笔交易，我们都做得了。只要出现，我们就能想出办法做成。我们不会这么做，但我们可以引入合伙人，给他们合伙关系中的优先份额。这大概率不会发生。但有很多事我们做得到。

所以，不要因为保险公司分红的法定限制，就排除任何一种可能——

**Charlie Munger**（02:01:43）：我们可以获得特别许可来——

**Warren Buffett**（02:01:45）：哦，我们可以获得——

**Charlie Munger**（02:01:47）：——宣派更多股利。你不应该认为我们被自然法则限制在现在的金额。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:58:51）：OK. Gregg?

**Gregg Warren**（01:58:53）：Warren, in this year's annual report it was noted, much as it is every year, that payments of dividends by the company's insurance subsidiaries are restricted by insurance statutes and other regulations, with Berkshire's insurance operations currently allowed to declare up to 16 billion as ordinary dividends during 2018. My question here is, should we view this annual regulatory threshold for dividends as a benchmark for allowable share repurchases as well? And in the event that Berkshire wanted to buy back more stock than that, or pay out even more as dividends, would there be an issue with you using capital from operations that aren't held by the insurance operations to return additional capital? With the side question here being, would the annual cash distribution from BNSF, which is held on National Indemnity's books, be excluded?

**Warren Buffett**（01:59:50）：Yeah. The — we will obviously follow the rules of the states in which we're domiciled, and all the rules, of course. But basically it's the state of domestication in the insurance companies, and they do restrict the amount of dividends in any given year. Although you could, if you wanted to, request some additional amount. But we don't ever consider that. But repurchases — if repurchases were really attractive, we would do it in a very big way. And, you know, I wouldn't rule — there's all kinds of ways that we could arrange things to do either a very large acquisition, which is what I would prefer, or a very large repurchase, would I don't think is probably in the cards just because the way our stock trades, not because we wouldn't like it at a large discount. So Charlie and I, we've got the appetite. And we would have — we've got a lot of cash — but we could have a lot more cash. We can make any deal of — even one of a very large size. We can make anything that came along — we could work out how to get it done. We would — we would have — we're not going to be doing this, but we would have partners who would come in and give us a preferential part of a partnership. That's not going to happen, in all probability. But there's a lot of things that we could do. So don't rule out anything based on statutory limitations of distributions from insurance companies —

**Charlie Munger**（02:01:43）：And that — we could get special permissions to —

**Warren Buffett**（02:01:45）：Oh, we can get —

**Charlie Munger**（02:01:47）：— declare bigger dividends. We are not — you should not assume that we're constrained by the laws of nature to the amount that we can take out under the statutes now.
