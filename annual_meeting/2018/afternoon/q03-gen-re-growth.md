## 问题

通用再保险增长不错、管理层有变动。是什么带来了增长和改善？

## 回答要点

1. 蒙特罗斯退休后在阿吉特之下重心转向增长，「但一切都有承保纪律」
2. 财产险再保险会有可观增长；寿险再保险国际业务增长尤其大
3. 去年 AIG 100 亿大单是史上最大，今年没有续做，净收入因此下降
4. 「五年、十年、二十年、五十年后我们仍在再保险业务里」
5. 芒格：「任何白痴金融家都能进入的那部分变得难多了。」

## 关键引述

> **“anything associated with Ajit is — also has underwriting discipline attached to it”**
>
> 「跟阿吉特有关的一切都附带着承保纪律」

**Warren Buffett**（00:10:34）：好，加里。

**Gary Ransom**（00:10:40）：关于再保险。我知道过去讨论过再保险未来十年不如过去十年有吸引力。但我们没专门谈过通用再保险（Gen Re）。今早看了 10-Q，看到通用再保险增长不错。管理层有些变动。能否谈谈公司发生了什么带来了增长和改善？

**Warren Buffett**（00:11:12）：对。再保险业务，我不觉得比十年前更难。但四五十年前竞争没这么残酷。通用再保险的塔德·蒙特罗斯为我们做了出色的工作，退休了。在阿吉特之下（还有卡拉·拉格尔），公司的重心有所变化。可能比以前更偏增长。但我向你保证，跟阿吉特有关的一切都附带着承保纪律。如你注意到的，确实有起色。财产险再保险业务会有可观的增长。寿险再保险——我们主要就在这块做寿险——自接管以来增长非常大，尤其是国际业务。这部分我喜欢。通用再保险的规模会稍微扩大。但我们有多种参与再保险的方式。我们做大额批量交易，这就是为什么今年净收入下降了。去年我们做了 AIG 那笔 100 亿的交易，史上最大。今年没有续做。在我看来，五年、十年、二十年、五十年后我们仍会在再保险业务里。

我们会有一些不寻常的优势，这既来自我们的资本状况、我们对这门生意的态度，也来自我们的人才。总体来说，我们的保险业务远好于平均水平。我们有一些真正的珍宝，外界对它了解甚少。我们还有非常好的再保险业务，它会比 GEICO 这类每年稳步向前的业务波动更大。但它会是伯克希尔很重要的一部分。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（00:13:53）：对。我要说，任何白痴金融家都能轻易进入的那部分变得难多了。怎么可能不难？

**Warren Buffett**（00:14:07）：查理是我岳父的替身——（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（00:10:34）：OK. Gary.

**Gary Ransom**（00:10:40）：Yes, on reinsurance. I know we've talked in the past about reinsurance not really being as attractive an industry in, say, the next ten years as the last ten. But I don't think we've talked specifically about General Re. And I looked this morning at the 10-Q and I see General Re has grown nicely. I know there's been some changes in the management. And I wondered if you could just give us a sense of what's going on at the company to bring about some of that growth and what looks like improvement.

**Warren Buffett**（00:11:12）：Yeah. Well, the reinsurance business — I don't think I'd say that it's tougher than it was ten years ago. But you go to 40 or 50 years ago, it was not brutally competitive, I'll put it that way. And at Gen Re — Tad Montross, who did a fantastic job for us at Gen Re, retired. And we have under Ajit [Jain] — and then Kara [Raiguel] in addition — but under Ajit, the focus of the place has changed somewhat. And it probably is more growth oriented than before. But I can assure you that anything associated with Ajit is — also has underwriting discipline attached to it. But, I — there has, as you've correctly noticed, there's been some pick-up. And I think you actually will see the property-casualty reinsurance business grow a fair amount. And the life business — reinsurance business — and this is really the only place we do much in life — but that has grown very substantially ever since we took it over, particularly internationally. And so that part, I like. And we will have a somewhat — I think we’ll have a somewhat larger operation at Gen Re. But we have various methods, as you know, of being in reinsurance. We do these huge bulk deals. That's why our net revenues are down this year. We did that $10 billion deal with AIG, which was the biggest deal in history, last year. And we don't have a repeat of it this year. We will be in the reinsurance business five years from now, 10 years from now, 20 years from now, and 50 years from now, in my view. And we will have some unusual advantages that stem both from our capital position, our attitude toward the business, and the talent that we have. We have an — we have a way better than average insurance business, generally. We have some real gems that nobody really knows much about. And we have a very, very good reinsurance business that will be subject to more ups and downs than something like GEICO will be, which just moves ahead every year. But it will be an important part of Berkshire. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（00:13:53）：Yeah. I would argue the part that any idiot financier can easily get into has gotten way tougher. And why wouldn't it?

**Warren Buffett**（00:14:07）：Charlie is my substitute for my father-in-law that was — (Laughter)
