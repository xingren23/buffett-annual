## 问题

能否用虚构数据展示确定内在价值的数学原理，包含思维模型、管理层评估和行业假设？

## 回答要点

1. 芒格：「我给不了公式化的方法，因为我不用。」
2. 「把所有因素混在一起，差距不够吸引人就换下一个」
3. 好市多 12—13 倍市盈率时「低得荒谬」——地产、竞争地位、人事制度都喜欢
4. 「想要公式的话，回研究生院去吧——他们会给你一堆不管用的公式。」
5. 巴菲特：「这是最久一次没听到查理说他更喜欢好市多。」

## 关键引述

> **“I can't reduce that to a formula for you.”**
>
> 「我没法给你归纳成公式。」

**Warren Buffett**（00:14:15）：好，11 号站台。

**Audience Member**（00:14:22）：嘿，沃伦。查理。再次感谢你们邀请我们。我无法充分感谢和欣赏你们为我们带来的成果和以身作则树立的原则。谢谢。（掌声）查理，你提到过如果给你同样的机会——只是资金规模更小——你仍会寻找定价错误的股票机会。

**Charlie Munger**（00:14:49）：当然。

**Audience Member**（00:14:50）：那显然是通过公司的内在价值来判断——即未来现金流的折现总和。你能不能用虚构的数据集演算一下、展示确定内在价值的数学原理？我希望你包含考虑关键指标的综合思维模型、对管理层的量化评估、以及对行业盈利能力持久性的假设。沃伦也是，能不能展示大资金规模下的算例，以及从小资金到大资金的变化？

**Charlie Munger**（00:15:36）：嗯，我给不了你公式化的方法，因为我不用。（笑）我只是把所有因素混在一起（笑），如果价值和价格的差距不够吸引人，就换下一个。有时纯粹是量化的。比如好市多（Costco）卖 12、13 倍市盈率时，我觉得那价格低得荒谬——因为这门生意的竞争优势太强了、太可能继续越做越好。我没法给你归纳成公式。我喜欢它的廉价地产。喜欢它的竞争地位。喜欢它的人事制度。什么都喜欢。虽然它是账面值的三倍左右，我觉得它值更多。但不是什么人——想要公式的话，回研究生院去吧。（笑）他们会给你一堆不管用的公式。（掌声）

**Warren Buffett**（00:16:57）：这是我们开伯克希尔年会以来最久一次没听到查理说他更喜欢好市多而不是伯克希尔。（笑）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（00:14:15）：OK, Station 11.

**Audience Member**（00:14:22）：Hey, Warren. Charlie. Thank you again for having us and having me. I just can't thank you guys enough and appreciate you guys enough for the body of work that you guys have delivered to us and the exemplar example that you guys have set with your principles. Thank you. (Applause) Charlie, you've mentioned that, if given the chance — or the same chance with a smaller capital base — you would still look for mispriced stock opportunities.

**Charlie Munger**（00:14:49）：Of course.

**Audience Member**（00:14:50）：And that would be determined through, obviously, what we've called the intrinsic value of the organization — or the company in question — an aggregate of the discounted future cash flows. Would you work the arithmetic using a fictional data set to illustrate the mathematical principia to determine an intrinsic value? And I hope you include the comprehensive mental model of the key metrics considered, and quantitative assessments of the management, and any assumptions of its industry to determine the durability of its earning power. And, Warren, same to that effect. Would you also demonstrate or illustrate an arithmetic problem set using, with a significant capital base, and provide the object lessons on how those have changed from a small to a large capital base?

**Charlie Munger**（00:15:36）：Well, I can't give you a formulaic approach because I don't use one. (Laughter) And I just mix all — (laughter) — I just mix all the factors and if the gap between value and price is not attractive, I go on to something else. And sometimes it's just quantitative. For instance, when Costco was selling at about 12 or 13 times earnings, I thought that was a ridiculously low value, just because the competitive strength of the business was so great and it was so likely to keep doing better and better. Well, I can't reduce that to a formula for you. I liked the cheap real estate. I liked the competitive position. I liked the way the personnel system worked. I liked everything about it. And I thought, even though it's three times book, or whatever it was then, that it's worth more. But that's not a formula that anybody — If you want a formula, you should go back to graduate school. (Laughter) They'll give you lots of formulas that won't work. (Applause)

**Warren Buffett**（00:16:57）：This is the longest we've ever gone in a Berkshire meeting without Charlie saying that — getting to the point where he prefers Costco to Berkshire. (Laughter)
