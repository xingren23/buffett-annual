## 问题

随着世界其他地方增长，是否看到更多美国以外的大型交易机会？

## 回答要点

1. 「有些失望」——以色列 ISCAR 是幸运的一笔
2. 美国以外的公司知道伯克希尔，「但不会自动打电话找我们」
3. 芒格：收购被杠杆收购和「战略」驱动——「我把它翻译成农场用语」
4. 「连杠杆收购玩家都在流鼻血」
5. 「我们从来没有战略计划——除非你瞒着我。」

## 关键引述

> **“We've never had a strategic plan unless you've hidden it from me.”**
>
> 「我们从来没有战略计划——除非你瞒着我。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:31）：加里。

**Gary Ransom**（01:41:35）：是的，您说过在寻找非保险的大型收购来投放现金。我通常想到的是美国，因为您是美国商业的拥趸。但随着世界其他地方开放和增长，您是否看到更多机会？比如亚洲或欧洲的大型交易？

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:05）：加里，我对此有些失望。我们确实在美国以外看到了一些——感谢上天我们多年前在以色列看到了那笔（ISCAR），当时艾坦·韦特海默给我写了封信。我们买了它，现在是伯克希尔非常重要的部分。但美国以外的公司当然知道伯克希尔，但不会自动拿起电话找我们。在美国，我认为人们……

**Charlie Munger**（01:43:57）：对，但企业收购游戏现在被杠杆收购和所谓的「战略」驱动——我通常把它翻译成农场用语。（巴菲特笑）价格对我们来说太疯狂了。我们很难做。杠杆收购玩家喜欢高杠杆、不介意高价——连他们都在流鼻血。这不容易……

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:38）：你做过一笔你记得的「战略」交易吗——

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:41）：我们得等。

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:43）：你记得我们做过一笔战略交易吗？

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:47）：嗯？

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:48）：我们做过一笔我们自己认为是「战略」的交易吗？

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:51）：我们从来没有战略计划——除非你瞒着我。（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:59）：好。这就回答了那个问题。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:41:31）：Gary.

**Gary Ransom**（01:41:35）：Yes, you've said that you are looking for non-insurance large acquisitions to put that cash to work. And when you've said that, I've usually thought of the United States, because you're a big fan of the U.S. business. And I just was wondering whether you're seeing more opportunities as the rest of the world opens up, grows, whether there's opportunities for some of those mega-transactions in other parts of the world, say Asia or Europe.

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:05）：Yeah, Gary, I would say that I've been disappointed in that, because we do see some outside the United States, and thank heavens we saw the one we saw in Israel some years ago [ISCAR] when Eitan [Wertheimer] wrote me a letter. But — and you know, we bought a business which is a very important part of Berkshire now. But we are still not — they're certainly aware of Berkshire Hathaway outside the United States. But they don't sort of pick up the phone automatically. In the United States, I think any large — particularly private — company that thinks — is thinking about doing something, they at least think about Berkshire. But that — in Europe or Asia, that — we are not embedded in the minds the same way. They know about us, they know we've got a lot of money and they know we like to buy things. But we really, we're on the radar screen big-time in the United States, and we're not as — we don't — the immediate desire to be sure that they've thought about the Berkshire option does not occur the same way outside the United States. And we've tried to encourage it a few ways. But I would say that the results have not been great at all. But I hope tomorrow, you know, I get a call from Germany or Britain or Italy or you name it, and Australia, wherever it may be. And I hope I get a call and we get an opportunity to do it. There's a good many countries we'd be quite happy to put substantial money into it. And like I say, our experience in Israel has been just terrific. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:43:57）：Yeah, but the corporate acquisition game now is so driven by the leveraged buyout and the so-called, whatever they call them, strategic, yes, strategic. I usually translate that into barnyard language. (Buffett laughs) And we're so — there's so much craziness in price from our viewpoint. Of course, it's very hard for us to do it. The people in the leveraged buyout game, who love massive leverage and don't mind high prices, even they are getting nosebleeds. It's hard. And it's not an environment that means that — that allows Berkshire just to go out and buy a whole lot of companies.

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:38）：Have you ever made a strategic deal that you —

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:41）：We have to wait.

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:43）：We've made a strategic deal that you can remember?

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:47）：Hmm?

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:48）：Have we ever made a deal that we would have regarded as strategic?

**Charlie Munger**（01:44:51）：We've never had a strategic plan unless you've hidden it from me. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:59）：OK. That answers that.
