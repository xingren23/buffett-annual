## 问题

回售 Phillips 66 股票后股价跳涨 22 美元。10% 持股门槛的因素是什么？长期怎么看？

## 回答要点

1. 「这就是去年的城市服务优先股」——93、94 卖出，后来 115
2. 越想监管和交易问题越坚定保持低于 10%；航空股同样处理
3. 用股票换了 Phillips Specialty Products 子公司——「我们喜欢那个子公司」
4. 「实际上我们在 Phillips 上做得不错。」

## 关键引述

> **“overwhelmingly we will stick below 10 percent on marketable security holdings”**
>
> 「越坚定地在可交易证券上保持低于 10%」

**Audience Member**（00:48:57）：我的问题是关于最近决定回售 Phillips 66 股票的。不是要让你们难堪。但之后股价跳了约 22 美元。您提到当时超过 10% 持股有一些监管要求。能否谈谈决定持股超不超 10% 门槛的因素？以及对 Phillips 66 的长期看法？

**Warren Buffett**（00:49:34）：是的，那就是去年的 City Service 优先股。（笑）我们大约在 93、94 美元卖了，现在大概是 115。我们持有那家公司略低于 10% 的股份。

泰德、托德和我越是琢磨与监管、交易相关的一堆问题，就越倾向于在可流通证券的持股上压在 10% 以下。航空股我们就是这么做的。这并不意味着我们会减持美国运通之类，绝对不是。

格雷格·加兰在 Phillips 66 做得非常出色，我们与公司关系非常好，他是一位经验非常丰富、非常明智的经理人。但我确实决定要把这笔持股降到 10% 以下。就像我说的，富国银行我们也会略低于 10%；在美国航空和联合大陆上，我们其实还卖了几股，就是为了保持在 10% 以下。除非出现特殊情况，我们会守在 10% 以下。

Phillips 我们持有 9% 多一点。我认为他们在经营上做得好，在资本配置上也做得好。几年前我们用一家企业换他们的股票，那家企业很不错，我们保留下来继续经营。所以我们在 Phillips 里还压着很多钱。我希望当初成交价格能更高些，但我们卖出的部分赚了钱，也实现了一个目标。查理？

**Audience Member**（00:48:57）：My question is about the recent decision to sell shares back of Phillips 66. Not to put you on the hot seat. But right after that, share prices jumped up about $22 a share. You mentioned at the time that there's some regulatory requirements if you own over 10 percent of a company. Could you talk about the factors that go into how you decide whether to retain more than that or get under that threshold? And then what are your thoughts long-term on Phillips 66, like, their business mid-stream refining?

**Warren Buffett**（00:49:34）：Yeah, well, it was the City Service preferred of last year. (Laughter) We sold the stock at around 93 or '4. And it's probably 115 now. But we own just under 10 percent of the company. And the more Ted [Weschler] and Todd [Combs] and I think about various problems connected with regulatory problems and trading problems and so on, overwhelmingly we will stick below 10 percent on marketable security holdings. We've done it with the airlines. Now that does not mean we're going to reduce our holdings in American Express or anything of the sort. But — and Greg Garland has done a great job at Phillips 66. We've had very good relations with the company. They're very — he's a very, very, very experienced and sensible manager. But I did decide that I wanted to be below 10 percent in that holding. And we, like I say, we'll stay just slightly under 10 percent of Wells Fargo. We've actually sold a few shares just to stay below 10 percent in the case, I think, of both American Airlines and United Continental. Unless there's something unusual we're going to stay under ten. But we have nine and a significant fraction percent of Phillips. And I think they've been good at operations. I think they've been good at capital allocation. We traded them a business — we traded them stock for a business some years ago, which has been a very nice business that we've retained in operation. So we've got a lot of money still in Phillips. And I wish I'd made the deal at a higher price. But we made money on what we sold and we accomplished an objective. Charlie?

**Warren Buffett**（00:51:56）：Yeah. Yeah, actually we've done pretty well with Phillips.
