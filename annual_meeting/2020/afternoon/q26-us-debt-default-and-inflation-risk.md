## 问题

旧金山股东 Charlie Wang 提问：鉴于当前经济和债务水平都前所未有，美国政府对其债券违约是否可能存在风险和后果？

## 回答要点

1. 用本国货币发行债券不会违约，货币购买力才可能出问题
2. 美国以本国货币发行债务，而阿根廷因以外币举债深陷困境
3. 巴菲特以「巴菲特币」和印钞机为例，说明本币债务永远不会违约
4. 标准普尔下调美国政府评级在他看来毫无道理，企业不可能比能印钞还债的主体更强大
5. 债务上限之争很荒谬，债务不会被还清只会被再融资；90 年代末曾短暂小幅下降
6. 诀窍在于始终用本国货币借款，国家的偿债能力也会随经济增长

## 关键引述

> **“The United States has been smart enough, and people have trusted us enough, to issue its debt in its own currency.”**
>
> 「美国足够聪明，人们也足够信任我们，让我们以本国货币发行债务。」

> **“It is very painful to owe money in somebody else's currency.”**
>
> 「用别人的货币欠债是非常痛苦的。」

> **“The debt isn't going to be paid, it's going to be refunded.”**
>
> 「这笔债务不会被还清，只会被再融资。」

**Becky Quick**（01:41:54）：好，这个问题来自旧金山的股东 Charlie Wang。他说：「考虑到经济和债务水平空前，美国政府债券违约会不会有任何风险和后果？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:15）：不会。如果你用自己的货币发债，货币会怎样，可能是个问题，但你不会违约。美国一直足够聪明，人们也足够信任我们，让我们用自己的货币发债。阿根廷现在出问题，就是因为它的债不是用自己的货币借的。很多国家都有过这个问题，将来还会有很多国家有。用别人的货币欠债是非常痛苦的。但如果你，听着，如果我能发行一种货币，巴菲特币，而且我有一台印刷机，你知道，我就能借钱，我能用那种货币借钱，我绝不会违约。（笑）所以，你最终拿到的购买力可能有疑问。但就美国政府而言，当年标准普尔下调美国政府评级时，我想是标准普尔，几年前，在我看来，那毫无道理。我是说，归根结底，你怎么能认为任何一家公司比一个能印钱付你的人更强，我实在不理解。所以，别担心政府违约。顺便说一句，我觉得设置这些债务上限之类的东西，然后让政府就是否提高上限吵个不停，有点疯狂，这事该说清楚。我们会上调债务上限的。这笔债不会还清，只会被借新还旧。任何以为自己能削减国债的人，我是说，那，你知道，有过短暂的时期，我想是九十年代末前后，债务确实降过一点。这个国家会印更多的钱，顺便说，这个国家的偿债能力也会增长。但，诀窍在于一直用自己的货币借债。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:41:54）：All right, this question comes from Charlie Wang. He's a shareholder in San Francisco. He says, "Given the unprecedented time of the economy and the debt level, could there be any risks and consequences of the U.S. government defaulting on its bonds?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:42:15）：No. The — if you — if you print bonds in your own currency, what happens to the currency is — it can be a question, but you don't default. The United States has been smart enough, and people have trusted us enough, to issue its debt in its own currency. And Argentina is now having a problem because the debt isn't in their own currency. And lots of countries have had that problem, and lots of countries will have that problem in the future. It is very painful to owe money in somebody else's currency. But if you — listen, if I could issue a currency — Buffett Bucks — and I had a printing press, you know, I could borrow money — and I could borrow money on that — I would never default. (Laughs) So, what you end up getting, in terms of purchasing power, can be in doubt. But in terms of the U.S. government — when Standard and Poor's downgraded the United States government — I think it was Standard and Poor's, some years back — that, to me, did not make sense. I mean, in the end, how you can regard any corporation as stronger than a person who can print the money to pay you, I just don't understand. So, don't worry about the government defaulting. I think it's kind of crazy, incidentally — this should be said — to have these limits on the debt and all of that sort of thing, and then stopped government arguing about whether it's going to increase the limits. We're going to increase the limits on the debt. The debt isn't going to be paid, it's going to be refunded. And anybody that thinks they're going to bring down the national debt, I mean — that — you know — there's been brief periods — I think in the late '90s or thereabouts — when the debt has come down a little bit. The country is going to print more — incidentally, the country is going to grow, in terms of its debt paying capacity. And — but the trick is to keep borrowing in your own currency.
