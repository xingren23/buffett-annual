## 问题

股东埃里克·勒凡特向格雷格提问。面对航空航天业的严重放缓，精密铸件公司（Precision Castparts）将如何应对？

## 回答要点

1. 精密铸件公司很大一部分业务来自航空航天业，按 10-Q 可分三个领域，其中两个（大型客机与支线喷气机）受冲击，国防合同业务依然稳健强劲。
2. 公司正根据波音的需求实时调整业务，每周与波音通话，确认生产订单后相应调整。
3. 波音一两天前刚融资 250 亿美元，此前还融资 140 亿美元；空客近期也表示看不清自身未来。
4. 真正的问题在于是否需要大量新飞机以及何时需要，这影响精密铸件、通用电气、波音等整条产业链。
5. 巴菲特指出需求枯竭并非这些公司自身造成，而是美国叫停航空旅行所致，严重程度取决于冲击波音的同类变量。

**Becky Quick**（00:34:42）：下一个问题来自 Eric LeFante，是问格雷格的：「精密铸件公司（Precision Castparts）如何应对航空航天业的严重放缓？」

**Greg Abel**（00:34:52）：这和刚才讨论的内容非常一致——航空航天业显然是它们业务的一大部分。大致可以分成三块，就像我们在 10-Q 里做的那样，其中两块受到影响。精密铸件的国防合同业务依然非常稳健、强劲。但如果看大型机身飞机，以及支线喷气机所用的飞机，这块业务会随需求、随长期订单直接变动。所以，精密铸件此刻仍在持续根据波音方面的需求调整业务。他们每周与波音开电话会，确认对方的生产订单，并据此调整。

**Warren Buffett**（00:35:46）：波音一两天前刚募了 250 亿美元，此前还募过 140 亿。一年前他们还觉得自己现金状况良好，我理解这一切是怎么发生的。空客也一样。它们最近一周还说过，自己其实并不知道未来会怎样。我也不知道未来会怎样。

这个国家会有飞机，我们会继续飞行。真正的问题是需不需要大量新飞机，以及大概什么时候需要。这影响到很多人，肯定也影响精密铸件、通用电气，显然还有波音。需求一下子枯竭，本质上是重重一击，而且会沿着产业链往上传。

但飞机制造商没把自己弄到这步田地，航空公司没有，精密铸件没有，通用电气也没有。根本上，是我们把这个国家的航空旅行关掉了。这对人们的习惯、对他们今后的行为意味着什么，很难评估。我不知道答案。但我们确实知道，它会对精密铸件产生影响，有多严重，取决于同样那些正在冲击波音的变量。

航空业里你随便点一家公司——航空是个大生意，顺便说一句，这个国家也擅长这个。想想波音，那真是一家了不起的公司，而且很重要：巨大的出口商，牵动大量就业，其中一些就在我们这儿。我们希望最好的结果，也祝愿所有人好运。显然，我们更希望自己没事。但其中一部分，肯定不在我们的掌控之中。

**Becky Quick**（00:34:42）：This next question comes from Eric LeFante, and it's directed to Greg. He asks, "How is Precision Castparts handling the severe slowdown in the aerospace industry?"

**Greg Abel**（00:34:52）：So, very consistent with everything we've just discussed, which is, obviously, a large part of their businesses, is the aerospace industry. And it can really be broken into three areas as we do in our (10-)Q, but two are being impacted. The defense contract business remains very sound and strong within Precision Castparts. But if you look at the large-body aircraft, the aircraft that they use within the regional jets, that business will move directly with the demand there, and the jets that are ordered longer term. So, Precision Castparts is, literally as we speak, continuing to adjust their business relative to the demand that would come out of Boeing. They would be having weekly calls with Boeing, recognizing what are their production orders there and adjusting the business accordingly.

**Warren Buffett**（00:35:46）：Boeing raised 25 billion just a day or two ago, and they raised 14 billion before that. And a year ago, they felt they were in a fine cash position, and I understand how all that happened. Airbus has had the same situation. They've made some comments recently, within the last week, you know, the fact that they — they really don't know what their future is. And I don't know what their future is. We're going to — we're going to have aircraft in this country. We're going to be flying. But the real question is, whether you need a lot of new planes or not, and when you'll — when you're likely to need them. And it affects a lot of people. And it certainly affects Precision Castparts. It affects General Electric. It, obviously, affects Boeing. And it's — it is a blow to, essentially, have your demand dry up. And it goes up in the chain. And, you know, the aircraft manufacturers didn't — they didn't bring it on themselves. The airlines didn't bring it on themselves. Precision Castparts didn't bring it on itself. General Electric didn't — It's, basically, that we shut off air travel in this country. And what that does to people's habits, how they behave in the future, it's just hard to evaluate. I don't know the answer. But we do know that it will have an effect on Precision Castparts. And how severe it will be depends on the same sort of variables that are hitting Boeing. You name the company in aircrafts — and aircraft's a big business. And this country's good at it, incidentally, too. I mean, if you think about Boeing, you know, it is one hell of a company and it's important. It's a huge exporter and it affects a lot of jobs. And some of them are with us. And, you know, we hope for the best and we wish everybody the best. Obviously, we wish ourselves the best in it. But part of it is out of our — certainly out of our control.
