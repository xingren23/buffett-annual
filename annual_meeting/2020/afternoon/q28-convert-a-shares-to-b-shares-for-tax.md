## 问题

即将退休的长期股东 Lou Bogart 说，他理解拆股不影响股票价值，但 A 股价格太高，退休提取现金时面临巨额税负。他举例：若 A 股均价约 30 万美元、每股有 20 万美元资本利得税负，仅需 6 万美元现金也得出售整股、承受 20 万美元税负。他问若 10 比 1 拆股，就能卖两股各 3 万美元、把税负降到 4 万美元并保留更多投资，伯克希尔是否考虑过？他后悔当初没买 B 股。

## 回答要点

1. A 股可直接转换为 B 股，巴菲特 7 月向五个基金会捐赠前就立即转换，让受赠方拿到 B 股
2. 14 年来他一直如此操作，B 股便于小额捐赠、花费或交易
3. B 股曾拆分过以降低面额、方便以更小单位交易
4. A 股投票权更多，但售价长期接近平价
5. 过去 A 股在股东指定捐赠计划中有优势，该计划早已取消，如今 A、B 股将被完全同等对待
6. 需要现金时可拿出一两股 A 股转成 B 股变现，很多人已经这样做

**Becky Quick**（01:51:09）：好，这是佛罗里达州博卡拉顿的 Lou——Lou Bogart 的问题。他说：「我是长期股东，临近退休，有个顾虑。我理解拆股对股票价值没有任何作用的理论。然而，A 股价格极高，当我在退休后想从组合里取出一些钱时，就面临巨额税负。假设今年均价约 30 万美元，每股我要承担 20 万美元的资本利得负债。如果退休时我额外需要 6 万美元现金，我就得卖掉整整一股，然后被征收 20 万美元的税。如果你按 10 比 1 拆股，我可以卖两股 3 万美元的股票，把税负控制在更容易承受的 4 万美元。我也能把更多投资继续留在伯克希尔。」他说：「你考虑过这个吗？回想起来，我当初该买 B 股，但当时没想到。」

**Warren Buffett**（01:52:02）：嗯，你可以把 A 股转成 B 股，这正是我 7 月向五家基金会捐款时所做的事。实际上，我在捐赠前立刻转换，我是说，这样他们拿到的是 B 股。事实是，B 股对那些想捐出一小部分持股、或花掉一点、或诸如此类的人非常有用。所以，你可以把 A 转成 B，这正是我 14 年来捐股一直在做的事，这样就，解决了那个问题，而且我们，我记得我们把 B 股做过一次拆分，从某个点位起，你知道，就是让它更容易操作，好让人们用更小的面额交易。B 股，A 股的表决权不同，但，我们前些时候通过了一项决议，我想，而且肯定是这样，无论如何，我们绝不会让 A 股比 B 股占优。过去在股东指定捐赠计划里，A 股曾有优势，那是我们一开始就设进去的，但那很久远了，现在没有了，所以 B 股和 A 股长期会被完全同等对待。A 股确实投票权更多，但，它们的售价一直非常接近平价。所以，我想说，如果你想做点什么来筹现金，而你有很多 A 股，你知道，你拿一两股 A，我认识的很多人都这么做，把它兑现，转成 B，然后取出你想要的任何现金数额。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Becky Quick**（01:51:09）：All right, here's a question from Lou — Lou Bogart in Boca Raton, Florida. He says, "I'm a longtime shareholder with a concern as I head into retirement. I understand the theory that splitting shares does nothing for the value of shares. However, with the extremely high price on A shares, when I wish to draw down some money on my portfolio in retirement, I'm facing a large tax hit. "Say the average price has been about $300,000 this year, and I'm sitting on a $200,000 capital gains liability for each share. If I need $60,000 in additional cash during my retirement, I need to sell a full share and get hit with $200,000 tax liability. "If you would split the stock 10-for-1, I could sell two $30,000 shares and keep my tax liability at a more manageable 40,000. I could also maintain more of my investment in Berkshire." He said, "Have you thought about this? In retrospect, I should have bought B shares but didn't think about it the time."

**Warren Buffett**（01:52:02）：Well, you can convert A to B shares, which is exactly what takes place when I give away the money in July to the five foundations. I actually convert it immediately before the gift, I mean, and so they get B shares. And the truth is, the B shares are very useful to people that want to, either, give away a small portion of what they have, or spend it, or whatever it may be. So, you can convert the A to B shares, which is exactly what I've been doing now for 14 years as I give it away, and — and solve that problem, and we — We split the B shares, as I remember it, from one point, you know, just to make it even more manageable, so that people could deal with smaller denominations. The B shares — the A shares have a different voting power — but, we passed some resolution some time ago, I think — and it certainly would be the case — in any event, we're never going to give the A shares an advantage over the B. They used to have an advantage in a shareholder-designated contribution program that we had, and we put that in there when we started but that's in — that goes way back in time, and that doesn't exist anymore, so that the B and the A are going to get treated exactly the same over time. It's true the A have more votes, but — and they sell — they sell very close to parity all the time. So, I would say that if you want to do anything, in terms of raising cash, and you've got a lot of A shares, you know, you take one or two shares of A — and plenty of people I know have done this — and just cash in, and turn it into B, and give yourself whatever amount of cash you want to get.
