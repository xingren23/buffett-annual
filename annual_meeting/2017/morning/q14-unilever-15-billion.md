## 问题

伯克希尔据报与 3G、卡夫亨氏联手，拟以 1430 亿美元收购联合利华。伯克希尔原本准备投多少？这是否意味着下一笔大型收购仍将与 3G 合作？

## 回答要点

1. 卡夫亨氏股权分散，伯克希尔与 3G 作为控制股东合计持股略超 50%
2. 按最初设想，若达成友好协议，伯克希尔与 3G 将各追加投资 150 亿美元
3. 交易还需卡夫亨氏独立董事批准；报价的前提是确保债务之外有足够股权资本
4. 伯克希尔只是非正式承诺这 150 亿美元，条件是必须为友好交易
5. 起初以为联合利华至少可能感兴趣，发现并非如此后即撤回报价

## 关键引述

> **“we would have invested an additional 15 billion and 3G would have invested an additional 15 billion”**
>
> 「我们会再投 150 亿美元，3G 也会再投 150 亿美元。」

> **“when we found out otherwise, we withdrew the offer”**
>
> 「等发现并非如此，我们就撤回了报价。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:11）：好，杰伊？

**Jay Gelb**（01:31:17）：据报道，伯克希尔与 3G、卡夫亨氏联手，试图以 1430 亿美元收购联合利华。伯克希尔原本愿意在这笔交易里投多少钱？这是否意味着伯克希尔下一笔大型收购很可能与 3G 合作？

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:34）：嗯，卡夫——这里要区分两种情况。卡夫亨氏是一家股权分散的公司，我们和 3G 作为控制股东，合计持有略超 50% 的股份。但按最初的设想（不确定最终是否就会这样），如果能达成友好协议，我们会再投 150 亿美元，3G 也会再投 150 亿美元。所以如果交易成立、卡夫亨氏的独立董事批准，很可能我们就投了 150 亿，但这需要独立董事同意。卡夫亨氏在推进这个报价时，希望确保除了将承担的债务之外，还有足够的股权资本让交易成立。所以我们基本上非正式地承诺了这 150 亿，但条件是必须是与联合利华的友好交易。起初我们认为对方至少可能感兴趣；等发现并非如此，我们就撤回了报价。

所以，很可能就是追加 150 亿美元。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:11）：OK. Jay?

**Jay Gelb**（01:31:17）：Berkshire reportedly partnered with 3G and Kraft Heinz's attempt to acquire Unilever for $143 billion. How much was Berkshire willing to invest in this deal? And does this mean Berkshire's next large acquisition is likely to be in partnership with 3G?

**Warren Buffett**（01:31:34）：Yeah, well, Kraft, I — you'd have to distinguish between two situations. Kraft Heinz was a widely-owned company in which we and 3G act as a control group and have a little over 50 percent of the stock. But as originally contemplated — no certainty that this exactly is what would have happened — we would have invested an additional 15 billion and 3G would have invested an additional 15 billion if a friendly agreement could have been reached. So if the deal had been made, if the independent directors of Kraft Heinz had approved the transaction, the likely — well, then the likelihood is that we would have invested 15 billion. But it would've required the approval of the independent directors as well. Now Kraft Heinz, in going forward with making that offer, wanted to be sure that there would be enough equity capital, in addition to the debt that would be incurred, to make the deal. And so, informally, we had basically committed the 15 billion. It only was approved on the basis that it be a friendly deal with Unilever. And initially, we thought they would be at least possibly interested in such a deal. And when we found out otherwise, we withdrew the offer. So it would have been 15 billion of additional money, in all probability.
