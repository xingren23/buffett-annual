## 问题

策略往往一直有效、直到失效。除了巨额保险损失，还有什么可能让伯克希尔这台机器失灵？

## 回答要点

1. 唯一的实质性永久风险是大规模杀伤性武器，而「我不认为那是低概率事件」
2. 衰退、萧条、恐慌、飓风、地震都有影响，某些情况下反而让伯克希尔表现更好
3. 真正危险的是核、化学、生物或网络攻击成功扰乱社会，而想干成这件事的人与组织不少
4. 凭借多元盈利来源、资产状况与文化，「我们大概是最后一批受影响的」
5. 芒格以英国石油一口油井爆炸为例：伯克希尔的子公司真正独立，母公司不会被单点事故拖垮
6. 「无论从哪个方面，伯克希尔的结构都是为了承受压力而设计的」；但真发生那种事，要担心的就不止股价

## 关键引述

> **“I can't think of anything that can harm Berkshire in a material, permanent way except weapons of mass destruction”**
>
> 「我想不出任何能以实质性、永久性方式伤害伯克希尔的东西——除了大规模杀伤性武器。」

> **“But I don't regard that as a low probability.”**
>
> 「而我不认为那是低概率事件。」

> **“In every way, Berkshire is structured to handle stresses.”**
>
> 「无论从哪个方面，伯克希尔的结构都是为了承受压力而设计的。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:08）：好，贝基。

**Becky Quick**（01:15:12）：这个问题来自汤姆·斯潘费尔纳（音），他希望被称为宾夕法尼亚的汤姆·斯潘。他说：「在生活、商业和投资中，策略往往一直有效，直到它失效。除了巨额保险损失之外，你认为还有什么可能让伯克希尔这台机器失灵？」

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:31）：我认为唯一的——

**Charlie Munger**（01:15:34）：好问题。

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:38）：对。嗯，如果发生某种变化，如果我们被某种外部因素感染、文化被大幅改变，被另一种思想入侵。但就实际而言，我想不出任何能以实质性、永久性方式伤害伯克希尔的东西——除了大规模杀伤性武器。而我不认为那是低概率事件。衰退、萧条、恐慌、飓风、地震，这些都会有影响，某些情况下我们甚至可能因为它们表现更好。但如果美国遭到一次成功的核、化学、生物或网络攻击（以攻击者的标准衡量算成功），而想干成这件事的人、组织甚至少数国家都不少，它可能把社会扰乱到伤害我们的程度。但我认为，凭借多元的盈利来源、资产状况、以及运行中的总体哲学（那种文化），我们大概是最后一批受影响的。可如果有人真能杀死数百万美国人、彻底扰乱社会，那就什么都说不准了。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:17:26）：嗯，我同意。那需要非常极端的事。

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:02）：对。

**Charlie Munger**（01:18:03）：就拿英国石油来说，一口油井爆炸就造成巨额损失。而伯克希尔这些子公司都是独立的，真正独立。如果某处发生一起可怕的事故，母公司不会（听不清）。我们当然会赔付，可能超过法律责任，但我们不是——某一家子公司的一起事故造成巨大损失，我们比大多数公司受到的保护更好。

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:03）：对。

**Charlie Munger**（01:18:04）：无论从哪个方面，伯克希尔的结构都是为了承受压力而设计的。

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:09）：这类事我们一直在想，从创业起就在想。但我确实不知道有哪家公司能承受更多的普遍逆境，甚至某些具体逆境。至于「大规模杀伤性武器会怎样」，那是我们无法预测的。可真要发生，要担心的事就不止伯克希尔股价了。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:08）：OK. Becky.

**Becky Quick**（01:15:12）：This question comes from Tom Spanfelner (PH). And he'd like to be called Tom Span from Pennsylvania. He says, "In life, business, and investing, strategies often work until they don't work. Other than a massive insurance loss, any thoughts on what could cause the Berkshire enterprise to not work?"

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:31）：I think the only —

**Charlie Munger**（01:15:34）：Good question.

**Warren Buffett**（01:15:38）：Yeah. Well, if there were some change, if we got some infection — outside agent of some sort that changed the culture in some major way, an invasion of different thought. But as a practical matter, I don't think anything — you know, and it's the things you can't think of — but I can't think of anything that can harm Berkshire in a material, permanent way except weapons of mass destruction. But I don't regard that as a low probability. It would take a recession, a depression, a panic, you know, hurricanes, earthquakes. They all would have some effect. And in some cases, it might even be that we would do better because of them. But if there were a successful — as measured by the aggressor — nuclear, chemical, biological, or cyber-attack on the United States — and there are plenty of people that would like to pull that off or organizations and maybe even a few countries — it could disrupt society to such an extent that it would harm us. But I think — with the variety of earning streams, with the asset positions, with the general philosophy at play — the culture — I think that we would be close to the last one affected. But if somebody figures out how to kill millions of Americans and totally disrupt society, then, you know, then all bets are off. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:17:26）：Well, I agree. It would take something really extreme.

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:02）：Yeah.

**Charlie Munger**（01:18:03）：And just take the question like — British Petroleum took a huge loss with one oil well blowing. And Berkshire has all these independent subsidiaries. And they really are independent. And the parent company is not (inaudible) if there's one horrible accident somewhere. We would tend to pay, of course, maybe more than our legal liability, but we are not — one accident in one subsidiary that caused a big lot of damage, we're better protected than most companies.

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:03）：Yeah.

**Charlie Munger**（01:18:04）：In every way, Berkshire is structured to handle stresses.

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:09）：It's the kind of thing we think about all the time. We've thought about it ever since we started. But I really don't know any company that can take more general adversity or even some specific adversities. But if you get into the "what could happen with weapons of mass destruction?" that is something we can't predict about. But if that ever happens, there'll be more to worry about than the price of Berkshire.
