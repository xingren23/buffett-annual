## 问题

每个人在不同年龄的梦想会不同。两位现在的梦想是什么？

## 回答要点

1. 芒格：「有时候我特别向往的时候会想：啊，要是能再回到 90 岁就好了」
2. 给年轻人的建议：「如果你真有什么想做的事，别等到 93 岁」
3. 巴菲特：要去找那份「即便不需要工作你也会接受」的工作，别把这种事往后推
4. 引克尔凯郭尔：生活必须向后被理解，却必须向前去活；人生需要做一点逆向工程
5. 芒格补一个葬礼笑话：牧师请人说逝者的好话，最后只有一人上前——「他弟弟更糟」

## 关键引述

> **“If you got anything you really want to do, don't wait till you're 93.”**
>
> 「如果你真有什么想做的事，别等到 93 岁。」

> **“look for the job that you would take if you didn't need a job”**
>
> 「去找那份即便不需要工作你也会接受的工作。」

> **“His brother was worse.”**
>
> 「他弟弟更糟。」

**Charlie Munger**（01:52:04）：我的梦想。嗯——（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:52:12）：第一个就别说了。（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:52:23）：有时候我特别向往的时候会想：啊，要是能再回到 90 岁就好了。（掌声）我给年轻人一点建议：如果你真有什么想做的事，别等到 93 岁。

**Warren Buffett**（01:52:44）：不，去做。（笑）这也是我对学生说的：你不一定第一次、第二次就找到它。但你走向世界时，要去找那份「即便不需要工作你也会接受」的工作。别把这种事往后推。有人——我想是克尔凯郭尔——说过：生活必须向后被理解，却必须向前去活。你要做一点逆向工程。这不意味着一切都能那样做，但你真该想想：等你老了，什么会让你的生活让你觉得满意，然后大体上朝那个方向走。当然，人生需要一点运气，路上也得接受一些坏事情。但生活对我和查理非常好，我们没什么可抱怨的。

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:52）：你不想成为那种人：葬礼上牧师说「现在，该有人来说说逝者的好话了」，结果没有人上前，没有人上前。他又说：「总该有人能说点逝者的好话吧。」还是没人。最后有一个人上来，说：「嗯，他弟弟更糟。」

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:14）：对。（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:52:23）：Sometime when I'm especially wishful, I think, oh, to be 90 again. (Applause) And I got some advice for the young. If you got anything you really want to do, don't wait till you're 93.

**Warren Buffett**（01:52:44）：No, do it. (Laughter) No, that's the same thing I would tell students is, you can't always find it the first time or the second time. But when you go out in the world, look for the job that you would take if you didn't need a job. I mean, don't postpone that sort of thing. Somebody — I think it was Kierkegaard, said that, you know, life must be evaluated backwards but it must be lived forwards. And you want to sort of — Charlie says all he wants to know is where he'll die so he'll never go there, you know. And so you — (Laughter) You do want to do a certain amount of reverse engineering in life. I mean, that's not — that doesn't mean you can do everything that way. But you really want to think about what will make you feel good, when you get older, about your life. And you, at least generally, want to keep going in that direction. And, you know, you need some luck in life. And you got to accept some bad things that are going to happen as you go along. But life has been awfully good to me and Charlie, so we have no complaints.

**Charlie Munger**（01:53:52）：What you don't want to be is like the man, when they held his funeral, and the minister said, "Now, it's the time for somebody to say something nice about the deceased." And nobody came forward. And nobody came forward. He said, "Surely, somebody can say somebody — something nice — about the deceased." And nobody came forward. And finally, one man came up. And he said, "Well," he said, "His brother was worse."

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:14）：Yeah. (Laughter)
