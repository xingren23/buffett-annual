## 问题

亚马逊因为贝索斯的才华极具颠覆性，芒格称他是「我们这一代的商业头脑」。巴菲特现在怎么看亚马逊？为什么伯克希尔没买入？

## 回答要点

1. 「因为我太笨了，没看出会发生什么」——尽管欣赏贝索斯很久，也没料到他能做到今天这个规模
2. 完全没想过亚马逊云服务与云计算这条路：零售扩张的同时还颠覆整个科技行业，当年在他看来是极不可能的事
3. 「我低估了他执行的才华」——1997 年年报里就把路线图写清楚，然后做到了，而且远远超出
4. 「它一直看起来很贵」，因此始终没有下手
5. 芒格：亚马逊做成的事非常困难，「它会不会这么成功，完全不是显而易见的」；他不为错过亚马逊感到遗憾
6. 芒格补一句「我认为我们有一点搞砸了」，指的是谷歌；巴菲特：「我们错过了很多东西……而且还会继续错过」

## 关键引述

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:00:21）：好，第四站。

**Audience Member**（01:00:25）：你好。我叫乔伊（音），是沃顿的 MBA 学生。谢谢你们让我们来。亚马逊因为杰夫·贝索斯的才华而极具颠覆性——查理之前称他是我们这一代的商业头脑。你目前怎么看亚马逊？为什么伯克希尔没买入？

**Warren Buffett**（01:00:47）：嗯，因为我太笨了，没看出会发生什么（笑）——尽管我很欣赏杰夫。我欣赏他很久了，也一直在看他做的事，但我不认为他能做到今天这个规模。我甚至没想过亚马逊云服务、云计算这种可能性。所以如果你当年问我：在他把零售做起来的同时，还会做出颠覆科技行业的东西，概率有多大？在我看来那是极不可能的事。我低估了——我真的低估了他执行的才华。在网上做这些事，梦想是一回事，能做到需要极大的能力。你可以去读他 1997 年的年报，他把路线图写得清清楚楚，然后他做到了，而且远远超出。如果你没看过三四个月前他在查理·罗斯节目上的访谈（CharlieRose.com），去听一下，你会学到很多，至少我学到了。

所以我就是……它一直看起来很贵，我真的从没想过他会走到今天这一步。我觉得他非常出色，但无论 3 年、5 年、8 年还是 12 年前我看的时候，都没想过他能到今天这里。查理，你是怎么错过的？（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:02:28）：很容易。（巴菲特笑）那里做成的事非常困难，而且它会不会这么成功，完全不是显而易见的。我对错过亚马逊的成就并不感到遗憾。但有些事情本来更容易。我认为我们有一点搞砸了。

**Warren Buffett**（01:02:55）：不。这条线我们不追。（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:00）：嗯，我指的是谷歌。

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:02）：嗯，我们错过了很多东西。

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:06）：是的。

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:07）：我们错过了很多东西。

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:09）：而且还会继续错过。

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:10）：对。（笑）我们会有两个——

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:15）：幸运的是，我们没有全部错过，沃伦。这就是我们的秘密。我们没有全部错过。（笑）

**Warren Buffett**（01:02:18）：好，我想我们该往下走了。（掌声）他可能要说具体的事了。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:00:21）：OK. Station 4.

**Audience Member**（01:00:25）：Hi. My name is Joey (PH). And I'm an MBA candidate at Wharton. Thank you for having us. Amazon has been hugely disruptive, due to the brilliance of Jeff Bezos, whom Charlie earlier called the business mind of our generation. What is your current outlook and — on Amazon? And why hasn't Berkshire bought in?

**Warren Buffett**（01:00:47）：Well, because I was too dumb to realize what was going to happen — (laughs) — even though I admired Jeff. I've admired him for a long, long time and watched what he was doing. But I did not think that he could succeed on the scale he has. And I certainly didn't — I didn't even think about the possibility of doing anything with Amazon Web Services or the cloud. So if you'd asked me the chances that, while he was building up the retail operation, that he would also be doing something that was disrupting the tech industry, you know, that would've been a very, very long shot for me. And I've underestimated — I've really underestimated the brilliance of the execution. I mean, it's one thing to dream about doing this stuff online, but it takes a lot of ability. And, you know, you can read his 1997 annual report. And he laid out a roadmap. And he's done it, and done it in spades. And if you haven't seen his interview on Charlie Rose three or four months ago — CharlieRose.com — go to it and listen to it because you'll learn a lot. At least, I did. So, I just plain — It always looked expensive. And I really never thought that he would be where he is today. I thought he would do — I thought he was really brilliant. But I did not think he would be where he is today when I looked at it three, five, eight, 12 years ago — whenever it may have been. Charlie, how did you miss it? (Laughter)

**Charlie Munger**（01:02:28）：It was easy. (Buffett laughs) What was done there was very difficult, and it was not at all obvious that it was all going to work as well as it did. I don't feel any regret about missing out on the achievements of Amazon. But other things were easier. And I think we screwed up a little.

**Warren Buffett**（01:02:55）：No. We won't pursue that line. (Laughs)

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:00）：Well, I meant Google.

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:02）：Well, we missed a lot of things.

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:06）：Yes.

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:07）：We missed a lot of things.

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:09）：And we'll keep doing it.

**Warren Buffett**（01:03:10）：Yeah. (Laughter) And we'll have a two —

**Charlie Munger**（01:03:15）：Luckily, we don't miss everything, Warren. That's our secret. We don't miss them all. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（01:02:18）：OK. We better move on, I think. (Applause) He may start getting specific.
