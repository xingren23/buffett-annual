## 问题

您说管 100 万美元能做到年化 50%。会用什么策略？雪茄烟蒂还是套利？

## 回答要点

1. 「很可能是套利策略」，但方式与常规套利大不相同
2. 「几乎没有风险的赚法，不用多少杠杆」
3. 「把 100 万换成 1 亿，那 50% 就会像石头一样坠落」
4. 芒格：大钱自己长锚——「见过一个又一个天才，转眼管了 300 亿，好成绩就没了」
5. 芒格举例：李·卢利光靠学生贷款的浮头寸，毕业后不久攒下近 100 万美元

## 关键引述

> **“you change the one million to a hundred million and that 50 goes down like a rock.”**
>
> 「把 100 万换成 1 亿，那 50% 就会像石头一样坠落。」

**Warren Buffett**（02:18:14）：9 号站。我们差不多——好，还有时间再取两问。

**Audience Member**（02:18:05）：约翰·多索（音，纽约）：我叫约翰·多索，来自纽约。巴菲特先生，您说过如果管理 100 万美元的组合，您可以做到年化 50%。您会用什么策略？是雪茄烟蒂——即以极便宜的价格买平庸生意？还是某种套利策略？谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（02:18:29）：很可能是套利策略，但方式可能与常规套利大不相同，而且大部分如此。我可以向你保证：如果查理或我管 100 万，我们能找到几乎没有风险的赚法，不用多少杠杆。但把 100 万换成 1 亿，那 50% 就会像石头一样坠落。

市场上确实有一些没人注意的边缘低效，偶尔给人机会，但对伯克希尔毫无适用性。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（02:19:17）：我完全同意。你以前常说，大钱会自己长锚，越做越难。我见过一个又一个天才，业绩辉煌，转眼管了 300 亿、楼下坐满两层年轻人，好成绩就没了。事情就是这样。

**Warren Buffett**（02:19:42）：当查理——

**Charlie Munger**（02:19:43）：钱越多越难。

**Warren Buffett**（02:19:45）：查理当律师时，最初还做过几个地产项目。如果你真想赚 100 万——或在 100 万上赚 50%——又肯下功夫，做得到。但对管理巨额资金毫无适用性。希望有，可惜没有。

**Charlie Munger**（02:20:12）：是。李·卢利（音）——全额奖学金毕业不久，光靠学生贷款的浮头寸就攒下了差不多 100 万美元。他只找到几件事可做，然后把它们做了。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:18:14）：Station 9. We're just about — yeah, we got time for a couple more.

**Audience Member**（02:18:05）：My name is John Dorso (PH), and I'm from New York. Mr. Buffett, you've said that you could return 50 percent per annum, if you were managing a one-million-dollar portfolio. What type of strategy would you use? Would you invest in cigar butts, i.e., average businesses at very cheap prices? Or would it be some type of arbitrage strategy? Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（02:18:29）：It might well be the arbitrage strategy, but in a very different, perhaps, way than customary arbitrages, a lot of it. One way or another, I can assure you, if Charlie was working with a million, or I was working with a million, we would find a way to make that with essentially no risk, not using a lot of leverage or anything of the sort. But you change the one million to a hundred million and that 50 goes down like a rock. There are little fringe inefficiencies that people don't spot. And you do get opportunities occasionally to do. But they don't really have any applicability to Berkshire. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:19:17）：Well, I agree totally. It's just you used to say that large amounts of money, they develop their own anchors. You're just — it gets harder and harder. I've just seen genius after genius with a great record and pretty soon they got 30 billion and two floors of young men and away goes the good record. That's just the way it works.

**Warren Buffett**（02:19:42）：When Charlie —

**Charlie Munger**（02:19:43）：It's hard as the money goes up.

**Warren Buffett**（02:19:45）：When Charlie was a lawyer, initially, I mean, you were developing a couple of real estate projects. I mean, if you really want to make a million dollars — or 50 percent on a million — and you're willing to work at it — that's doable. But it just has no applicability to managing huge sums. Wish it did, but it doesn't.

**Charlie Munger**（02:20:12）：Yeah. Lee Louley (PH), using nothing but the float on his student loads, had a million dollars, practically, shortly after he graduated as a total scholarship student. He found just a few things to do. And did them.
