## 问题

前进保险正夺取最多市场份额。GEICO 计划如何应对，保住第二大车险公司的地位？

## 回答要点

1. 贾因拆解：费用率 GEICO 领先约 7 个点，赔付率落后约 12 个点，净落后约 5 个点
2. GEICO 正全力弥合赔付率差距，「有时我们领先，有时前进保险领先」
3. 一季度净增约 34 万份保单，利润率约 9 个点
4. 巴菲特敢下重注：未来五年 GEICO 份额会增加，今年肯定会
5. 「某种程度上这是场两马竞赛，而我们这匹马非常好」；芒格补刀：总有人偶尔比我们强一点

## 关键引述

> **“it's a two-horse race, and we've got a very good horse.”**
>
> 「某种程度上这是场两马竞赛，而我们这匹马非常好。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

**Warren Buffett**（00:26:03）：杰伊。

**Jay Gelb**（00:26:08）：杰伊·盖尔布（巴克莱保险分析师）：问题关于 GEICO。前进保险（Progressive）凭直营与独立代理双渠道、以及车险房险打包销售，正在主要车险公司中夺取最多的市场份额。GEICO 计划如何应对竞争威胁，保住第二大车险公司的地位？希望也能听听阿吉特或 GEICO 管理层的看法。谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（00:26:46）：前进保险经营得非常好，GEICO 也经营得非常好。我认为很长一段时间里，其余车险公司将难以避免向这两家丢失份额。长期以来我一直认为前进保险经营出色，他们对增长有胃口；有时他们学我们一点，有时我们学他们一点，五年、十年后仍会如此。

我们也销售大量房主保险，走代理安排。我们曾自营商主险，直到安德鲁飓风——当时公司还不归我们控制——之后决定放弃自营：房主险一年亏掉的可能等于之前 25 年赚的，而浮存金又不大。所以我们变成一家把客户的房主险需求转给几家大型稳健机构的公司。大头是车险。

一季度我们净增约 34 万份保单，除了前进保险，这个数字会比谁都好看；比去年多不少，但不如前年。利润率在 9 点上下。所以我对 GEICO 感觉极好——托尼（奈斯利）和团队建起的一切是完美的。我们不持有前进保险，但它是极好的公司，我们看它怎么走，它看我们怎么走。五年、十年后见分晓：我们俩谁先超过州农保险（State Farm）。查理？哦，阿吉特你要说吗？

**Ajit Jain**（00:29:39）：阿吉特·贾因：不太技术化地说，承保利润由两大变量决定：费用率和赔付率。费用率上 GEICO 对前进保险有约 7 个点的显著优势。赔付率上，前进保险比 GEICO 做得好，大约领先 12 个点。净下来，前进保险领先约 5 个点。GEICO 非常清楚自己在赔付率上的劣势，正全力尽快弥合。几个项目在推进中，有时 GEICO 领先，有时前进保险领先——现在是前进保险领先。我希望他们在赔付率上赶上来，同时守住费用率优势。谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（00:30:35）：自托尼（奈斯利）接手以来，GEICO 基本上每年都在夺取份额——具体数字我得核实。我敢下重注：未来五年 GEICO 的市场份额会增加，今年肯定会。这是一门极好的生意——而前进保险也是极好的生意。如阿吉特所说，我们在费用上有优势，并将保持优势；问题是会不会把那 5 到 6 个点的优势在赔付率上还回去。我们在非常努力地改进，相信他们也在努力改进系统。某种程度上这是场两马竞赛，而我们这匹马非常好。

**Charlie Munger**（00:31:52）：但沃伦，事物的本性就是：总有人偶尔在某件事上比我们强一点。

**Warren Buffett**（00:31:52）：哈，你注意到了。（笑声）

**Charlie Munger**（00:31:57）：是，我注意到了。

**Warren Buffett**（00:31:58）：很多业务上，第二名我也能接受。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（00:26:03）：Jay.

**Jay Gelb**（00:26:08）：This question is on GEICO. Progressive is gaining the most market share among the major auto insurers, based on its presence in the direct and independent agency channels, as well as now bundling its auto and homeowners insurance coverage. How does GEICO plan on responding to competitive threats so that it can retain its place as the second-largest auto insurer? I was hoping we could also hear on this topic from Ajit (Jain) or GEICO's management. Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（00:26:46）：OK. Progressive is a very well-run business. GEICO is a very well-run business. And I think they will, for a long time, be the two companies that the rest of the auto insurance industry has trouble not losing share to. But there's, you know, I think — I've always thought for a long, long time, Progressive has been very well run. They have an appetite for growth. Sometimes they copy us a little, sometimes we copy them a little. And I think that'll be true five years from now and 10 years from now. And we sell substantial amounts of homeowners insurance. We have an agency arrangement with that. We were in the business of writing it ourselves, until Hurricane Andrew, when a decision was made — we didn't control it then — but the decision was made that the homeowners, essentially, you could lose as much in one year as you made in the previous 25 years. And the float isn't as large. So, we became a company that placed our customers' desire for homeowners with several other large and solid organizations. The big thing is auto insurance. And we grew in the first quarter about 340,000 policies, net, which will look quite good compared to anybody but Progressive. And that was quite a bit more than last year, but not as good as two years ago. And the profit margin was in the nine-point area. So, I feel extremely good about GEICO, I mean, what has been built there by Tony (Nicely) and his people is perfect, but I would feel fine — We don't own any Progressive, but I think that Progressive is an excellent company, and we will watch what they do, and they will watch what we do. And we will see, five years from now or 10 years from now, which one of us passes State Farm first. Charlie? Oh, and Ajit, would you like?

**Ajit Jain**（00:29:39）：Well, the underwriting profit is really a function of two major variables. One is the expense ratio and the other is the loss ratio, without getting too technical. GEICO has a significant advantage over Progressive when it comes to the expense ratio, to the extent of about seven points or so. On the loss ratio side, Progressive does a much better job than GEICO does. They have, I think, about a 12-point advantage over GEICO. So, net-net, Progressive is ahead by about five points. GEICO is very aware of this disadvantage on the loss ratio that they are suffering, and they're very focused on trying to bridge that gap as quickly as they can. They have a few projects in place, and, you know, sometimes GEICO is ahead of Progressive. Right now, Progressive is ahead of GEICO. But I'm hopeful they'll catch up on the loss ratio side and maintain the expense ratio advantage as well. Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（00:30:35）：GEICO has gained market share, essentially — I'd have to look at the figures for sure — but virtually every year since Tony took over. And I would bet significant money that GEICO increases its market share in the next five years. And I think it will, for sure, this year. So, it is a terrific business. And — but Progressive is a terrific business. And we'll — as Ajit says, we've got the advantage in expenses, and we will have an advantage in expenses. And then the question is, are we — They have a very sophisticated way of pricing business. And the question is whether we give some of that five points back — or six points back — in terms of loss ratio. We are working very hard at that, but I'm sure they're working very hard too to improve their system. So, it's a — to some extent it's a two-horse race, and we've got a very good horse.

**Charlie Munger**（00:31:52）：But Warren, in the nature of things, every once in a while, somebody's a little better at something than we are.

**Warren Buffett**（00:31:52）：Ha. You've noticed. (Laughter)

**Charlie Munger**（00:31:57）：Yeah. I noticed.

**Warren Buffett**（00:31:58）：Yeah. I'd settle for second place in a lot of the businesses.
