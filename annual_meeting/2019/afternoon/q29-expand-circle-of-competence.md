## 问题

世界变化越来越快，我们该持续扩展能力圈，还是坚守现有圈子、冒着可选标的萎缩的风险？

## 回答要点

1. 「无论什么条件下你都应该扩展——如果能的话」；芒格：不能的话，非常谨慎
2. 强求不来；芒格打趣让巴菲特去跳芭蕾当领舞
3. 有时学到的是「我不胜任」——扔掉那一块也是收获
4. 向沃尔特·斯科特、格雷格·阿贝尔学到能源业务，但「远不到他们的水平」
5. 「不要因为觉得必须扩展而开始弯曲规则」

## 关键引述

> **“you should expand your circle of competence”**
>
> 「你都应该扩展能力圈」

**Warren Buffett**（02:14:50）：好，8 号站。（笑声）

**Audience Member**（02:14:59）：雅各布（音，来自中国的股东，哥伦比亚商学院毕业生）：你们好，沃伦。你好，查理。我叫雅各布，来自中国的股东，也是哥伦比亚商学院骄傲的毕业生。感谢邀请我们。（欢呼）我的问题是：世界今天的变化比 40 年前快得多，未来还会更快。在这种情况下，我们每个人是否应该随时间持续扩展能力圈？还是坚守现有圈子、冒着可选标的萎缩的风险？谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:38）：显然，无论什么条件下，你都应该扩展能力圈——

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:43）：如果能的话。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:45）：如果能的话。（笑声）这些年我扩展了一点……

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:55）：如果不能，我会非常谨慎。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:57）：是。强求不来。如果你告诉我必须成为物理学专家……

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:04）：或者去跳芭蕾当领舞，沃伦。那会是一景。（笑声）

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:08）：嗯，好吧。这倒是我没想过的……

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:10）：现在就可以开始。（听不清）

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:14）：那可能是你在想的事，我——（笑声）——压根没想到过。（笑声）不过，那就荒唐了。这不是说你完全不能扩展。这些年我在一些生意上学到了一些东西。有些时候我学到的是「我不胜任」——这其实是好事，那就把那一块扔掉。

世界会变，还会继续变，每天都在变，这让一切有意思。随着变化，在你自认为的现有能力圈内，你必须做它的主人——否则那根本不是你的能力圈。如果有机会顺势扩展——我和沃尔特（斯科特）、格雷格（阿贝尔）共事时学到了一些能源业务——但我远不到他们的水平，只是比过去懂得多了。

通常，核心能力大概与你的思维方式天生契合。有些人有我所谓的「金钱头脑」，在特定类型的金钱事务上如鱼得水。这不完全是智商问题。人的头脑很奇怪，各有专长——棋、桥牌不一而足。我见过能做在我看来不可能之事的人，用查理的话说，他们在其他领域又蠢得很。（笑）

所以，继续下功夫，但不要因为觉得「必须扩展」而开始弯曲规则。查理，还有吗？

**Charlie Munger**（02:18:13）：没有。

**Charlie Munger**（02:15:55）：If you can't — I'd be pretty cautious.

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:57）：Yeah. You can't force it. You know. If you told me that I had to, you know, become an expert on physics or, you know —

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:04）：Dance maybe the lead in a ballet, Warren. That would be a sight. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:08）：Yeah, well. That's one I hadn't really —

**Charlie Munger**（02:16:10）：(Inaudible) now.

**Warren Buffett**（02:16:14）：That's one you may be thinking about, but I— (laughter) — it hadn't even occurred to me. (Laughter) But, you know, it's ridiculous. That doesn't mean you can't expand it at all. I mean, I did learn about some things as I've gone along in a few businesses. In some cases, I've learned that I'm incompetent, which is actually a plus, then you've discarded that one. But it doesn't really — the world is going to change. And it's going to keep changing. It's changing every day. And that makes it interesting. You know. And as it changes, certainly within what you think is your present existing circle, you have to — you should be the master of figuring that one out or it really isn't your circle of competence. And if you get a chance to expand it somewhat as you go along — I've learned some about the energy business from Walter (Scott) and Greg (Abel) as we've worked together, but I'm not close to their level of competence on it. But I do know more than I used to know. And so, you get a chance to expand it a bit. Usually, I would think normally your core competence is probably something that sort of fits the way the mind has worked. Some people have what I call a "money mind." And they will work well in certain types of money situations. It isn't so much a question of IQ. The mind is a very strange thing. And people have specialties, whether in chess or bridge. I see it in different people that can do impossible — what seem to me — impossible things. And they're really kind of, as Charlie would say, stupid in other areas — (laughs) — you know. So just keep working on it. But don't think you have to increase it and therefore start bending the rules. Anything further, Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:18:13）：No.
