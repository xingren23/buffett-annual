## 问题

中国上周宣布 12 项金融业对外开放新措施。怎么看？外资未来会否在中国股市拥有更多定价权？有在中国设公司的计划吗？

## 回答要点

1. 芒格：DQ 冰雪皇后已开遍中国，「我们没等新法律，旧法律下就干了」
2. 巴菲特：本来有一件事若成规模会相当可观；「中国是个大市场，我们喜欢大市场」
3. 受规模所限，大概只能在 15 个左右的国家大规模部署资本
4. 芒格：「中美相处得好符合双方利益；处不来会双方都愚蠢」
5. 「我们在中国干得不错，只是干得还不够」

## 关键引述

> **“It really makes sense for the two countries to get along.”**
>
> 「中美两国相处得好，真的符合双方利益。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:10）：3 号站。

**Audience Member**（01:18:17）：Sasha 西西（音，中金公司）：你们好，巴菲特先生、芒格先生。我是中金公司的 Sasha 西西。上周中国宣布 12 项金融业对外开放新措施，让更多投资机构进入中国金融市场，并确保外资政策与内资一致。你们怎么看这些新措施？你认为外国金融机构未来会在中国股市拥有更多定价权吗？你们有在中国设立公司的计划吗？如果有，什么时间？谢谢。

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:19）：我们现在就有——DQ 冰雪皇后开遍中国。（笑声）运转良好。我们没等新法律，旧法律下就干了。不过我们在中国的净敞口不算大，对吧，沃伦？

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:32）：我们什么不算大？

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:37）：在中国。

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:38）：不算大，不过本来有一件事要是成了，规模会相当可观。中国是个大市场，我们喜欢大市场。受规模所限，我们大概只能在 15 个左右的国家大规模部署资本。

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:58）：但总体上，我认为气候在变好。中美两国相处得好，真的符合双方利益。想想中美如果处不来会多么愚蠢——顺便说，双方都愚蠢。

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:14）：是的。我们在中国干得不错，只是干得还不够。（掌声）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:18:10）：Station 3.

**Audience Member**（01:18:17）：Hello, Mr. Buffett and Mr. Munger. I'm Sasha Xixi (PH) from China International Capital Corporation Limited. Last week, China announced 12 new measures further opening up the financial industry. All these measures will allow more invested institutions to enter into Chinese financial market, and to insure the policies of foreign investment to be consistent with those of domestic investment. What do you think about these new measures? Do you believe the foreign financial institutions will have more pricing power over the Chinese stock markets in the future? Do you have any plans to set up a company in China? If so, what time? Thank you.

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:19）：Well, we've got one now. Dairy Queen is all over China. (Laughter) And it's working fine. And we didn't wait for new laws. We did it under the old laws. But we're not that big, net, in China, right, Warren?

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:32）：We're not that big what?

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:37）：In China.

**Warren Buffett**（01:19:38）：No, but we had something, you know, that could have happened that would have been quite sizable. But China, it's a big market, and we like big markets. I mean, we really can only deploy capital in a major way maybe in 15 or so countries just because of the size.

**Charlie Munger**（01:19:58）：But generally, I think the climate is getting better. It really makes sense for the two countries to get along. Think of how stupid it would be if China and the United States didn't get along. Stupid on both sides, I might add.

**Warren Buffett**（01:20:14）：Yeah. We've done well in China. We haven't done enough, but — (Applause)
