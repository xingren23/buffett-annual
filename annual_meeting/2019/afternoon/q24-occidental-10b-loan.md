## 问题

伯克希尔以 8% 优先股加认股权证为西方石油收购阿纳达科提供 100 亿美元融资。吸引力何在？未来会否有更多此类大型融资交易？

## 回答要点

1. 「不认为这是最后一单」；希望下一笔更大
2. 护城河是速度与确定性：周五来电、周六约见、周日搞定，无委员会、无重大不利变更条款
3. 「如果世界上存在任何 100 亿、200 亿甚至 500 亿的两天交易，他们拨号时一定会想到伯克希尔」
4. 罗恩·奥尔森十分钟确认无利益冲突；Munger, Tolles（洛杉矶）＋ Cravath（纽约）＋马克·汉堡周末加班
5. 西方石油董事会周一晚 10 点批准、周二清晨宣布——「有伯克希尔，他们就能做到」

## 关键引述

> **“they'll think of Berkshire Hathaway for sure in terms of what number to call.”**
>
> 「他们拨号时一定会想到伯克希尔·哈撒韦。」

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:39）：好，杰伊？

**Jay Gelb**（01:53:42）：杰伊·盖尔布（巴克莱保险分析师）：我问一个西方石油的问题，奇怪还没人问过。伯克希尔已承诺以 8% 股息率的优先股加认股权证，为西方石油收购阿纳达科提供 100 亿美元融资。这是自 2013 年亨氏收购以来伯克希尔首次承诺如此大规模的优先股投资。这笔交易的业务吸引力何在？未来是否应预期更多大型融资交易，作为伯克希尔配置多余现金的一种方式？

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:23）：我不认为西方石油交易是我们做的最后一单。（笑）也许一个月后就来一单，也许三四年后来一单。不会一模一样，我希望更大。关键是：这样的电话多半会打给我们，因为有些事我认为没有任何机构做得到——他们要过委员会，要「重大不利变化」条款，要这要那。而如果有人需要一大笔确定的钱，他们知道：周五下午打给我，周六约见，周日搞定。他们确定拿到 100 亿，我们也不会对交易结构指手画脚。

未来还会有类似（不必相同）的情形，那时电话会打给我们。我希望超过 100 亿。未来五年我们可能做很多类似的大额交易，也可能什么都没有。但如果世界上存在任何一笔 100 亿、200 亿甚至 500 亿的「两天交易」，请相信我，他们拨号时一定会想到伯克希尔·哈撒韦。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:56:15）：我喜欢。（笑声）

**Warren Buffett**（01:56:20）：成交之后我第一个电话打给查理——不对，先打给了罗恩·奥尔森，担心他可能有利益冲突。大约十分钟他就回话了。我告诉他必须周一晚前完成，西方石油那边同时也在给 Cravath 律所下同样的死命令。周一深夜，所有文件就绪。Munger, Tolles 在洛杉矶，Cravath 在纽约，我在奥马哈。我没干太多活，马克·汉堡做了大量工作——周日下午我去见西方石油的人时，他还在办公室忙别的。

这是理解我们、理解我们运作方式的人的产物，双方都有动力投入全部人力。他们的董事会周一晚 10 点开会批准，周二早晨宣布——这正是他们想要的。有伯克希尔，他们就能做到。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:53:39）：OK, Jay?

**Jay Gelb**（01:53:42）：Actually, I'm going to ask something about Occidental Petroleum. I'm surprised it hasn't been asked yet. So, Berkshire has committed to providing $10 billion in financing in the form of an 8 percent preferred share and attached warrants for Occidental's proposed acquisition of Anadarko. This is the first time Berkshire has committed to such a large preferred share investment since the acquisition of Heinz in 2013. What did you find attractive about the Occidental deal, in terms of its business? And should we expect other large financing transactions in the future, as a way for Berkshire to deploy a portion of its excess cash?

**Warren Buffett**（01:54:23）：I don't think the Occidental transaction will be the last one we do. (Laughs) There may be one, you know, in a month. They may be one three or four years from now. It won't be identical. I hope it's larger. But the point is, we're very likely to get the call because we can do something that really I don't think — no institution can do it. I mean, they've got committees that have to pass on it, and they want to have so-called MAC clauses, Material Adverse Changes. They want to do this and that. And if somebody wants a lot of certain money for a deal, you know, they've seen that I can get a call on Friday afternoon, and they can make a date with me on Saturday, and on Sunday, it's done. And they absolutely know that they have $10 billion and we're not going to tell them how to structure their transaction or do anything else. They've got it. And there will be times in the future when something, not identical, but similar, comes along, and we're the one to call. And I hope its larger than 10 billion. But — It could be — it could be we'll do — you know, in the next five years. It could be we'll do a lot of money, additional money in things similar to this, not identical. And it could be that nothing will happen. But if there are any $10 billion, or $20 billion, or maybe even $50 billion two-day transactions, if there are any in the world, believe me, they'll think of Berkshire Hathaway for sure in terms of what number to call. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:56:15）：Well, I like it. (Laughter)

**Warren Buffett**（01:56:20）：I called Charlie as soon as we made the — I called Ron Olson first because I was worried that he might have a conflict. And in about ten minutes, he had — I told him we — it had to be done by Monday night. And Cravath was being told the same thing by Occidental. And it was very late on Monday light, but all the papers were put in order. And Munger, Tolles was in Los Angeles, and Cravath was in New York, and I was in Omaha. And I didn't do that much; Mark Hamburg did a lot of the work. He was at work on Sunday on other things when I went down (laughs) to meet with the Occidental people. And it was the product of people who understood us, understood how we operate, and both with an incentive to put all the manpower necessary on the job. And like I say, I think their board of directors met at 10 o'clock on Monday night to approve it. But they could announce it Tuesday morning, and that's what they wanted to do. And with Berkshire, they could do it.
