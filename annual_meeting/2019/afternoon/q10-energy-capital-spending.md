## 问题

电力需求未来十年或因电动车、数据中心等回升，而伯克希尔能源的年开支预计从约 60 亿降到 2021 年的约 40 亿。哪些领域需要投入更多资本？没有布局的区域怎么办？

## 回答要点

1. 持中美洲能源近 20 年从未分红，其他公用事业公司「没有我们这样的资本胃口」
2. 阿贝尔：PacifiCorp 扩张计划 2008 年启动，目前只建成第一个三分之一，2021、2023 年陆续落地
3. 艾奥瓦布局已满：每来一个新数据中心，就再建 300 兆瓦可再生能源
4. 「按发电量相对客户用电量之比，全美国没有人排在我们前面」；电价约为最强对手的一半
5. 「我们会在能源上投入很多钱」——就在股东会场河对岸卖电，电价仍比公营的内布拉斯加便宜

## 关键引述

> **“We will put a lot of money into energy.”**
>
> 「我们会在能源上投入很多钱。」

**Warren Buffett**（00:45:38）：好，格雷格。

**Gregg Warren**（00:45:42）：格雷格·沃伦：沃伦、查理，美国电力需求过去十年停滞，但未来十年可能回升——电动车充电、数据中心和大麻种植三大新兴需求预计将占美国总电力需求的 5% 以上。这波需求多半会落在南大西洋、中西、西部和山区各州。最大受益者，将是为电网扩容、智能网络、可靠性与可再生能源投资的公司。伯克希尔能源在资本投入上一向激进，且在竞争区域已拥有一些最低的电价。但公司似乎正在缩减年度开支，此时恰恰可能需要更多投入：年开支预计从平均约 60 亿美元降到 2021 年的约 40 亿，其中三分之二偏维护而非增长。未来十年，伯克希尔能源是否需要在某个领域投入更多资本，以确保抓住这波需求增长——就像它已经在艾奥瓦西部风电上做到的那样（那里现在布满数据中心）？而在需求增长预期最强、但伯克希尔没有布局的区域，除收购外还有什么让资本发挥作用的途径？

**Warren Buffett**（00:47:01）：我马上把这个问题交给格雷格。先说一句：伯克希尔·哈撒韦能源有三个股东，我们占 91%。没有哪三个股东比我们更乐于把资金投入公用事业里的合理项目，也没有哪三家在人才上更能把握机会。持有中美洲能源近 20 年来，我们从未拿过一分钱股息。其他公用事业公司派高额股息——他们根本没有我们这样的资本胃口。所以问题只是找到合理的项目。我可以说，没有哪个团队比我们的团队更聪明、更有动力。那么交给格雷格。

**Charlie Munger**（00:48:03）：简而言之，我们已经好到不能再好，你该去担心点别的。

**Warren Buffett**（00:48:05）：（笑声）不过格雷格，请你站起来谈谈——我们真心希望在能源上花很多钱。

**Greg Abel**（00:48:16）：格雷格·阿贝尔：是的，下午好。格雷格，你点到了关键。往后看 2021、2022 年，要现实一些。正如你所说，2019、2020 收尾时我们的项目组合很充实，重点是在艾奥瓦建新的可再生能源项目、扩张电网。其他公用事业公司同样有机会。

艾奥瓦的布局实际已经比较满了。正如沃伦提到的，每来一个新数据中心，就意味着我们可以再建 300 兆瓦的可再生能源，我们会继续做。再看 PacifiCorp——我们在西北六州运营——扩张计划才刚起步：第一步是建重要的输电线路、扩电网，然后开始建可再生能源。给你一个监管节奏的概念：这个项目 2008 年启动，现在实际在建的是第一个三分之一。第二、第三阶段的规划已经就位，2021 年和 2023 年会陆续落地。在 NV Energy（内华达能源）也会照此推进：既有电网扩张以便调度资源，再辅以可再生能源。

正如你所说，具体项目还没最终确定，所以没有放进我们对外披露的资本预测里。等项目敲定、确认要开工，你们会看到增量资本——那是我们代表伯克希尔股东投入并赚取回报的资本。谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（00:50:10）：我们会在能源上投入很多钱。（笑）

**Charlie Munger**（00:50:16）：是，我们在这个领域状态好得不得了。

**Warren Buffett**（00:50:23）：顺便说，沃尔特·斯科特看到这些项目就兴奋，会跑去看现场。他对这门生意懂得极多——他忘掉的部分都比我将要知道的多。我们有极好的合作伙伴关系，有无限的资本，未来也会有。这个行业需要巨额资本。所以我认为，十年或二十年后人们回看我们的业绩，能源行业里不会有第二个这样的案例。

**Charlie Munger**（00:51:03）：格雷格，在能源上，艾奥瓦有没有谁排在我们前面？

**Greg Abel**（00:51:10）：查理，现实地说，放眼全美国都没有人排在我们前面，更别说艾奥瓦。按发电量相对客户用电量之比，我们确实领跑全国和艾奥瓦。

**Charlie Munger**（00:51:23）：我们的电价是不是还只有艾奥瓦最强竞争对手的一半左右？

**Warren Buffett**（00:51:28）：大约一半，接近。

**Greg Abel**（00:51:29）：正是这个区间。

**Charlie Munger**（00:51:31）：如果这还不能让你满意，我们就帮不了你了。（笑声）

**Warren Buffett**（00:51:39）：顺便说，我们就在离这里五英里的地方卖电。格雷格，对吗？

**Greg Abel**（00:51:46）：河对岸就是。

**Warren Buffett**（00:51:48）：对，河对岸。风照吹。内布拉斯加州从乔治·诺里斯时代起就是公营电力州，电力领域不存在资本主义。他们可以发行免税债券，我们只能发应税债券，成本因此高出一截；他们有大笔结余，不用付股息。可我们的电价基本还是比他们便宜。我们为公用事业业务感到非常自豪。

**Gregg Warren**（00:45:42）：Warren and Charlie, U.S. electricity demand has flatlined during the past decade, but could potentially pick up over the next decade with three emerging sources of demand — electric vehicle charging, datacenters, and cannabis cultivation — expected to account for more than 5 percent of total U.S. electricity demand. Utilities will have to work hard to benefit from this new demand, though, much of which is likely to accrue to states in the South Atlantic, Central, West, and Mountain regions, with the greatest benefit going to firms that invest in grid expansion, smart networks, reliability, and renewable energy. While Berkshire Energy has been aggressive with its capital investments, and already has some of the lowest electricity rates in the areas where it competes, it seems like the firm is winding down its annual spending at a time when more might actually be required. With annual spending expected to fall from around 6 billion, on average, annually to around 4 billion in 2021, with two-thirds of that spending being more maintenance driven than growth. Is there any one area where you feel Berkshire Energy might need to commit more capital over the next decade to ensure that it captures this future expected demand growth, much as it already has with wind power in western Iowa, which is now populated with a lot of data centers, and for territories where demand growth is expected to be the strongest but where Berkshire does not have a presence, are there any avenues aside from acquisitions for the company to put capital to work?

**Warren Buffett**（00:47:01）：I'm going to throw that over to Ajit in just a second. But I will tell you that we have three owners of Berkshire Hathaway Energy. We are the 91 percent owner. And there are no three owners that are more interested in pouring money into sensible deals within the utility industry or are better situated in terms of the people we have to maximize any opportunities. We have never had a penny of dividends in — whatever it is — close to 20 years of owning MidAmerican Energy. And other utility companies pay high dividends. They really — they just don't have the capital appetite, essentially, that we do. So, it's just a question of finding sensible projects. And I would say that there's no group that is as smart about it, as motivated about it as our group. And with that I'll turn it over to Greg.

**Charlie Munger**（00:48:03）：In short, we're about as good as you can get, and you should worry about something else.

**Warren Buffett**（00:48:05）：Yeah. (Laughter) But Greg, could you stand up and talk about —? We really hope to spend a lot of money in energy.

**Greg Abel**（00:48:16）：Yeah, yeah. Afternoon. Yeah, Gregg, you touched on it. A couple critical areas we go forward is to look realistically in the '21, 2022 timeframe. But equally, we do have those opportunities in our other utilities. The footprint in Iowa, realistically, is getting pretty full. As we hit a hundred percent renewables — Warren touched on it — every time we get a new data center, that means we can build another 300 megawatts of renewables. We'll continue to do that. But when you look at PacifiCorp, where we serve six states in the Northwest, we've really just embarked on an expansion program there. The first part was to build significant transmission, so expand the grid, and then start to build renewables. But we do have the planning in place for the second phase and the third phase, and that's what you'll see coming into place in 2021 and in '23. And the reality is we'll continue to do that at NV Energy, with really, again, the focus being on both grid expansion, so we can move the resources, and then supplementing it with renewables. So, it's exactly what you've touched on. And we haven't identified the specific projects yet, so we never put them in our capital forecast that we disclose to folks. But as they firm up and we know that they will go forward, clearly you'll see some incremental capital. And that's capital we clearly earn on behalf of the Berkshire shareholders as we deploy it. Thank you.
