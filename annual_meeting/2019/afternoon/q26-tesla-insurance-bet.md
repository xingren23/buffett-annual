## 问题

特斯拉将凭车辆数据自营保险，定价更准。这对 GEICO 意味着什么？纯线上卖车又对伯克希尔汽车经销业务意味着什么？

## 回答要点

1. 「车企做保险的成功概率，大概和保险公司造车的成功概率差不多」
2. 保险上真正的对手是前进保险，而非车企
3. 不怀疑车载数据的价值，但不认为车企因此有优势
4. 新车毛利约 6%，线上卖车腾挪空间不大
5. 「我不认为它会摧毁那些善待客户、提供服务的经销商」

## 关键引述

> **“the success of the auto companies getting insurance business are probably about as likely as the success of the insurance companies getting into the auto business.”**
>
> 「车企做保险的成功概率，大概和保险公司造车的成功概率差不多。」

**Warren Buffett**（02:02:52）：安德鲁？

**Andrew**（02:02:55）：安德鲁·罗斯·索尔金：应该是我的最后一问，虽然是两问，但同一主题。马斯克说特斯拉将给自己的车提供保险，因为它从路上所有车辆收集数据，定价会比普通保险公司更准。你谈过自动驾驶对保险业的威胁，那么车企亲自下场做保险，对 GEICO 意味着什么？另一个相近话题：特斯拉宣布转向纯线上销售，多家传统经销商也在缩减物业持有，因为购车者越来越多用手机和互联网选车。这对伯克希尔·哈撒韦汽车意味着什么？

**Warren Buffett**（02:03:43）：通用汽车旗下曾长期有一家汽车保险公司，各家车企都试过。我可以说：车企做保险的成功概率，大概和保险公司造车的成功概率差不多。（笑声）就保险业务而言，我担心的对手是前进保险，而不是我能看到的所有车企。这行一点不容易。我敢打赌：任何车企都不会在保险业取得非同寻常的成功。

用车联网研究驾驶习惯的做法正在普及，掌握人们怎么开车、刹车多狠、变道多猛的数据很重要。我不怀疑数据的价值，但我不认为车企在这方面有优势，也不认为它们能在保险业赚钱。

用互联网买车，就像用互联网买一切东西——多一个竞争对手而已。人们当然会寻找更好的方式。新车的毛利大约 6%，腾挪空间不大。但线上会卖掉一些车。我不认为它会摧毁那些善待客户、提供服务的经销商。不是压倒性的威胁，但显然会长期存在并卖掉一些车。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（02:05:46）：又是：没有。（笑声）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:02:52）：Andrew?

**Andrew**（02:02:55）：I believe it's my final question and admittedly it's a two-parter, but it's the same topic. Elon Musk says that Tesla will start to offer insurance for its cars and can price it better than a typical insurance company because of the data it collects from all of the vehicles on the road. You've talked about the threat of autonomous vehicles on the insurance business. But what about to GEICO of automobile themselves getting into the insurance business? And on a very similar topic, Tesla recently announced that they are shifting to an online-only sales model. And several traditional auto dealerships are also reducing their property holdings as car buyers increasingly use smart phones and the internet to shop for cars. What does this portend for Berkshire Hathaway Automotive?

**Warren Buffett**（02:03:43）：Yeah. Actually, General Motors had a company for a long time called Motors Insurance Company and various companies have tried it. I would say that the success of the auto companies getting insurance business are probably about as likely as the success of the insurance companies getting into the auto business. (Laughter) I worry much more about Progressive than all of the auto company possibilities that I can see, in terms of getting insurance business. It's not an easy business at all. And I would bet against any company in the auto business being any kind of an unusual success. The idea of using telematics, in terms of studying people's drivers habits, that's spreading quite widely. And it is important to have data on how people drive, how hard they break, how much they swerve, all kinds of things. So, I don't doubt the value of the data but I don't think that the — the auto companies will have any advantage to that. I don't think they'll make money in the insurance business. Using the internet to shop for cars is like, you know, using the internet for shopping for everything. It's another competitor. And there's no question that people will look for better ways. The gross margin on new cars is about 6 percent or thereabouts. So, there's not lots of room in the game. But that will be a method and that will sell some cars. And if there are, you know — it's another competitor. But I don't think it destroys the auto dealer who takes good care of the customers and is there to service the customers. It's not an overwhelming threat, but it's obviously something that's going to be around and will sell some cars. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:05:46）：Again, nothing. (Laughter)
