## 问题

据巴菲特说，泰德与托德加入以来略跑输标普 500。他们做了什么改变来提高未来十年跑赢的概率？

## 回答要点

1. 截至 3 月 31 日：一位小幅领先、一位小幅落后；「这是我最后一次就此作总结报告」
2. 托德深度参与伯克希尔—摩根大通—亚马逊医疗计划
3. 泰德主导 Home Capital 救援：周一听说、周三递出要约，稳定了一家被挤兑的加拿大金融机构
4. 「他们干得比我好」——打理自己的钱和别人钱都聪明
5. 「是在投标普也能赚很多钱的市场里赚的钱」

## 关键引述

> **“they're doing better than I am anyway.”**
>
> 「反正他们干得比我好。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:45）：5 号站。

**Auidience Member**（01:44:49）：艾伦·兰尼（魁北克蒙特利尔 Medici 组合经理）：我叫艾伦·兰尼，是魁北克蒙特利尔一家叫 Medici 的公司的组合经理。这个问题想提给托德（康布斯）和泰德（韦施勒）。据沃伦说，你们加入伯克希尔以来略跑输标普 500。最近你们做了什么改变，来提高未来十年各自股票组合跑赢标普的概率？

**Warren Buffett**（01:45:26）：我不确定托德或泰德今天在不在场。我来告诉你，这也是我最后一次就此作总结报告：截至 3 月 31 日，一位小幅领先，一位小幅落后。但他们是极出色的经理人。跑赢标普在这段时期不容易。

他们还在方方面面帮助伯克希尔。托德为医疗计划做的事——与摩根大通、亚马逊合作的那项——我不知道他每周投入多少小时。泰德在 Home Capital Group 上的作为：我们实质上以主要力量稳定了一家遭受挤兑、正在流血的加拿大金融机构——整件事都是他办的。我周一听说，周三我们就递出了收购要约。此前那家公司被几十拨人翻来覆去地审视，处境艰难。他做成的事令人惊叹，我想多伦多那边也领情。

所以有他们两位，我们好太多了。至于那个衡量指标——我就这么说吧：反正他们干得比我好。如果你要我总是汇报他们，那我就得连自己也一起汇报，和他们一比我就难堪了。（笑）他们干得更好，非常非常聪明。多年来打理自己的钱聪明，打理别人的钱也聪明。他们为我们赚了很多钱——不过是在一个投标普也能赚很多钱的市场里赚的。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（01:47:40）：不，我挺好。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（01:44:45）：Station 5.

**Auidience Member**（01:44:49）：Yes, hi. My name is Aaron Lanni. I'm a portfolio manager at a company called Medici out of Montreal, Quebec. My question is actually for Todd (Combs) and Ted (Weschler), if possible. So according to Warren, you lagged slightly behind the S&P 500 since joining Berkshire. So what recent changes, if any, have you implemented to increase your odds of beating the S&P in your respective stock portfolios over the next 10 years?

**Warren Buffett**（01:45:26）：Yeah, I'm not sure whether Todd or Ted are here, but they — I will tell you, but then — I'll make this the final report on it. But on March 31st, actually, one is modest ahead, one is modestly behind. But they are extraordinary managers. It has not been — it was a tough — it's been a tough period to beat the S&P and, like I say, one of them is now ahead of the S&P over that period, one's modestly behind. They've also helped us in just all kinds of ways. What Todd has done in connection with the medical initiative, we have — with J.P. Morgan, Amazon, I mean, I don't know how many hours a week he's worked totally on that. The things they've brought to me, what Ted did in terms of the Home Capital Group where we have essentially, in a major way — well, we stabilized a financial institution that was under attack and experiencing runs in Canada. And he did the whole thing. I heard about that on a Monday, and on Wednesday, we put an offer before the company. And previously to that, they probably had dozens and dozens of people combing over them and, meanwhile, they were struggling. And, you know, it was remarkable what he did and I think it's appreciated in the Toronto area. So, we are enormously better off because the two are with us. And while we have that measurement, like I say, I'll just put it this way, they're doing better than I am anyway. So, if you ask me to report on them all the time, I'll have to report and myself all the time, and I'm not — (laughs) — that would be embarrassing compared to how they do. They do better. They're very, very smart. They've been smart with their own money over the years. They've been smart in running other people's money over the years. And they've made us a lot of money, but they made it during a market where you'd have made a lot of money in S&P as well. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（01:47:40）：No, I'm fine.
