## 问题

你们现在最珍视人生中的什么？

## 回答要点

1. 芒格抢答：「我想要再多一点。」（指时间）
2. 「有两样东西买不到：时间和爱」
3. 当年想要钱，就是为了能随心所欲地生活——不是为了六栋房子或游艇
4. 「对两个身体渐老的老人来说，这是完美的工作」：体能无关紧要
5. 查理一周读的书比巴菲特一个月还多，还设计学生宿舍

## 关键引述

> **“It's the two things you can't buy, time and love.”**
>
> 「有两样东西买不到：时间和爱。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:23）：9 号站。

**Audience Member**（00:05:32）：罗布·李（音，加拿大温哥华）：你们好，沃伦和查理。我叫罗布·李，来自加拿大温哥华。能否与我们分享你们现在最珍视的东西？谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:50）：嗯——

**Charlie Munger**（00:05:51）：我想要再多一点。（笑声与掌声）

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:57）：有两样东西买不到：时间和爱。我很久以来就珍视它们。我在人生里极其幸运，能把对自己的时间控制到极致。查理也一直珍视这一点。我们当年真正想要钱，就是为了能随心所欲地生活——不是为了六栋房子或游艇。好吧，查理有艘船，但没给我们带来多少好处。

时间是宝贵的。我们非常幸运，身处一份体能无关紧要的工作——对两个身体渐老的老人来说，这是完美的工作。我们每天做着自己热爱的事。我几乎买得起钱能买的一切，而我做现在的事比做任何事都快乐。查理在设计学生宿舍，他的生活有意思，也为之注入很多。他一周读的书比我一个月读的还多，而且都记得住。

我们的境遇很好，但时间不是无限的。把时间省下来做喜欢的事，我们都把它做到了极致。

**Charlie Munger**（00:07:29）：一个人若能把自己的时间都花在真正喜欢的事情上，就是幸运的。那是福分。

**Warren Buffett**（00:07:44）：是的，我们一生好运多得让人难以置信。从出生在美国开始。顺便说，出生在加拿大也不错，我不想冒犯任何人。（笑声）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:23）：Station 9.

**Audience Member**（00:05:32）：Hi, Warren and Charlie. My name is Rob Lee (PH) from Vancouver, Canada. Could you please share with us what you value the most in life now? Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:50）：Well —

**Charlie Munger**（00:05:51）：I'd like to have a little more of it. (Laughter and applause)

**Warren Buffett**（00:05:57）：It's the two things you can't buy, time and love. And that, I value those for a long time. And I've been very, very, very lucky in life, in being able to control my own time to an extreme degree. Charlie's always valued that, too. That's why we really wanted to have money, was so we could do what we damn pleased, basically — (laughs) — in our life. It wasn't six houses or boats or anything. Well, Charlie's got a boat. But it doesn't do us that much good. But time is valuable. And we are very, very lucky to be in jobs where physical ability doesn't make any difference. And, you know, we've got the perfect job for a couple of guys with aging bodies. And we get to do what we love to do every day. I mean, I literally could do anything that money could buy, pretty much. And I'm having more fun doing what I do than doing anything else, and Charlie is designing dormitories. And I mean, he's got an interesting life, and he brings a lot to it. He still reads, you know, more books in a week than I get done in a month, and he remembers what he reads. So, we've got it very good, but we don't have unlimited time. And whatever we do to free up the time to do what we like to do — and we both maximize that in our lives — we do.

**Charlie Munger**（00:07:29）：Anybody's lucky if he so that what he spends his time at, he really likes doing. That's a blessing.

**Warren Buffett**（00:07:44）：Yeah, we've had so much good luck in life. It sort of blows your mind. Starting with being born in the United States. And Canada would be fine too, incidentally. I don't want to offend anybody. (Laughter)
