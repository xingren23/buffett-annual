## 问题

精密铸件的税前利润率比收购前低了近 10 个百分点。下滑主要是暂时性因素，还是行业竞争格局已变到难以大幅回升？

## 回答要点

1. 承认低于几年前的预期，但盈利已有所改善
2. 航空制造初期利润偏低是常态：学习曲线与产量爬坡需要时间
3. 盈利中含每年约 4 亿美元收购摊销，「在我看来那是经济意义上的盈利」
4. 「我的预期是，精密铸件的盈利会显著改善」
5. 「明年你还会问我这个问题，我想那时我的答案会不一样」

## 关键引述

> **“the earnings of Precision will improve fairly significantly.”**
>
> 「精密铸件的盈利会显著改善。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:00）：乔尼？

**Jonathan Brandt**（02:14:58）：乔纳森·勃兰特：精密铸件（PCC）的税前利润率放到全美工业里完全拿得出手，却仍比收购前几年低了近 10 个百分点，我猜也低于定价收购时的测算。年报暗示：计划外停工、新机型的学习曲线、油气产能转向航空航天，可能都在暂时压制利润率。但对这个业务合理的长期利润率预期是什么，并不清楚。我知道你们不愿给出具体目标或预测，但希望回答这个问题：2015 年以来的盈利下滑主要是暂时性因素，还是行业竞争格局与 PCC 的客户关系已变到「从当前水平大幅提升不大可能」的地步？

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:49）：你的铺垫很准确，确实低于我们几年前的预期。我的预期——一年前我也会这么说，而盈利已有所改善——基于我们手上的合同，以及航空制造业任何产品初期利润都偏低的事实：学习曲线和产量爬坡都要时间。我的预期是，精密铸件的盈利会显著改善。

我记得去年对你说过：它的盈利里有每年约 4 亿美元的收购摊销，在我看来那是经济意义上的盈利——如果它独立经营，会照此报告；我们因收购而不再单列。即便加上这 4 亿，它的盈利也仍低于我一两年内的预期。这是我目前的判断。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（02:17:14）：我没有要说的。

**Warren Buffett**（02:17:38）：明年你还会问我这个问题，我想那时我的答案会不一样。

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:00）：Jonny?

**Jonathan Brandt**（02:14:58）：Precision Castparts' pre-tax profit margins, while perfectly fine relative to American industry as a whole, continue to be almost 10 percentage points below where they were in the years preceding the acquisition. And I'm guessing they're lower than contemplated when the purchase price was determined. The annual report hints that unplanned shutdowns, the learning curve on new plane models, and a shift of oil and gas capacity to aerospace, might all be temporarily depressing margins. But it's unclear what a reasonable, long-term margin expectation is for this unit. Now, I know you won't want to issue a specific margin target or forecast, but I do have a question that I hope you can answer. Is the downward trend in earning since 2015 mostly due to these transitory items, or have the competitive structure of the industry and Precision's relationship with its customers changed to the point that meaningful increases from current margin levels are probably unlikely?

**Warren Buffett**（02:15:49）：Yeah. Your prelude is quite correct. I mean, they are below what we projected a few years ago. And my expectation — but I would have told you this a year ago — and they have improved somewhat. My expectation is, based on the contracts we have and the fact that the initial years in anything in the aircraft industry, for example, tend to be less profitable as you go further down the learning curve and the volume curve, tend to be lower in the near-term. My expectation is that the earnings of Precision will improve fairly significantly. And I think I mentioned maybe to you last year, in those earnings, there is about $400 million a year of purchase amortization, which are economic earnings in my viewpoint. So — but even including that 400 million a year, which they would be reporting if they were independent, and we don't report, because we bought them and there's a purchase amortization charge. Even without that, they are below what I would anticipate by a fair margin within a year or two. That's the present expectation on my part. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:17:14）：No, I don't have anything.

**Warren Buffett**（02:17:38）：You'll have that question for me next year, and I think I'll be giving you a different answer.
