## 问题

苹果已是第一大持仓。如何看待 Spotify 反垄断诉讼与伊丽莎白·沃伦参议员拆分应用商店的提议？这些批评公允吗？

## 回答要点

1. 「苹果让我难受的是股价涨了」——更愿意股价低一点好多买
2. 回购的算术：按 200 美元注销 5 亿股，按 150 美元注销 6.67 亿股，后者让持股比例更高
3. 「很大一部分收益会在我们一分钱不掏的情况下提升我们的持股比例」
4. 股价大涨长期反而对伯克希尔不利；不会向潜在竞争买方剖析全部预期
5. 芒格：苹果手机是家人最后一样愿意放弃的东西

## 关键引述

> **“what hurts, in the case of Apple, is that the stock has gone up.”**
>
> 「苹果让我难受的是股价涨了。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:22）：好，我们再取一题，然后午餐休会。安德鲁。

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（02:38:22）：安德鲁·罗斯·索尔金：谢谢沃伦。问题关于科技与公司第一大持仓。「苹果现在是我们的第一大持仓，请多谈谈你的想法。怎么看待它面临的监管挑战？Spotify 在欧洲以反垄断为由起诉苹果；伊丽莎白·沃伦参议员提议终结苹果对应用商店的控制——这将冲击其做大服务业务的战略。这些批评公允吗？」

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:53）：你说的这些我都知道，而且我非常满意我们的苹果持仓——它就是第一大持仓。说真的，苹果让我难受的是股价涨了。我不是提议做什么，但我们更愿意股价低一点，好多买。

重要的是：苹果前几天又批准了 750 亿美元的回购——假设未来三年它要花 1000 亿美元回购自家股票。账很简单：按 200 美元回购，能注销 5 亿股；现在流通 46 亿股，剩下 41 亿股。按 150 美元回购，能注销 6.67 亿股，总股本降到 40 亿股以下，我们持股的比例反而更高。也就是说，很大一部分收益——至少已获授权的部分——会在我们一分钱不掏的情况下提升我们的持股比例。对一门好生意来说，我太喜欢这个了。

股价大涨的新局面，长期看反而对伯克希尔不利。我认为我们仍然会干得不错，但我们不会向可能明天就和我们抢买的人剖析我们对苹果的全部预期——免费的投资建议我们不送。你提到的那些我们都知道，我们还有一大堆其他变量在共同起作用。我们是第一大持仓这件事，我们喜欢。查理？

**Charlie Munger**（02:41:23）：在我家，用苹果手机的人，手机是他们最后一样愿意放弃的东西。（笑声）

## 英文原文（CNBC 官方逐字稿）

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:22）：OK, we'll take one more, and then we'll break for lunch. Andrew.

**Andrew Ross Sorkin**（02:38:22）：Thank you, Warren. Question on technology and the company's biggest holding now. "Given that Apple is now our largest holding, tell us more about your thinking. What do you think about the regulatory challenges the company faces, for example? Spotify has filed a complaint against Apple in Europe on antitrust grounds. Elizabeth Warren has proposed ending Apple's control over the App Store, which would impact the company's strategy to increase its services businesses. Are these criticisms fair?"

**Warren Buffett**（02:38:53）：Well, again, I will tell you that all of the points you've made I'm aware of, and I like our Apple holdings very much. I mean, it is our largest holdings. And actually, what hurts, in the case of Apple, is that the stock has gone up. You know, we'd much rather have the stock — and I'm not proposing anything be done about it — but we'd much rather have the stock at a lower price so we could buy more stock. And importantly, if Apple — I mean, they authorized another 75 billion the other day — but let's say they're going to spend a hundred billion dollars in buying in their stock in the next three years. You know, it's very simple. If they buy it at 200, they're going to get 500 million shares. They've got 4 billion, 600 million out now. And so they'll end up with 4.1 billion under that circumstance. If they're buying at 150, they buy in 667 million shares. And instead of owning what we would own in the first case, we'd now — the divisor would be less than 4 billion, and we'd own a greater percentage of it. So, in effect, a major portion of earnings — at least possibly, it's at least been authorized — will be spent in terms of increasing our ownership without us paying out a dime, which I love for a wonderful business. And the recent development, when the stock has moved up substantially, actually hurts Berkshire over time. We'll still do — In my opinion, we'll do fine, but we're not going to dissect our expectations about Apple, you know, for people who may be buying it against us tomorrow or something of the sort. We don't give away investment advice on that for nothing. But we have — all the things you've mentioned, obviously we know about, and we've got a whole bunch of other variables that we crank into it. And we like the fact that it's our largest holding. Charlie?

**Charlie Munger**（02:41:23）：Well, in my family, the people who have Apple phones, it's the last thing they'll give up. (Laughter)
