## 问题

你常在信里谈伯克希尔对股东的「盈利能力」。按你的估算，现在的盈利能力是多少？未来十年会怎么走？

## 回答要点

1. 巴菲特的回答是「被下调了」：**公用事业（BHE）出了状况，直接拖累了基础盈利能力**——这与他此前对 BHE 估值下修的表述一致。
2. 他没有给出新的数字，只说明盈利能力的增长依赖「周期性出现的大交易」：像那笔没做成的 100 亿美元交易，「不做它还能为什么做？」
3. 换句话说，伯克希尔的 earning power 不是平滑增长的曲线，而是**「基础盘 + 偶发大机会」**的组合。

## 关键引述

> **“I think our underlying earning power was affected negatively here a while back by what happened in the utility field.”**
>
> 「我认为我们的基础盈利能力前一段时间受到了公用事业领域所发生事情的负面影响。」

> **“On the one that was 10 billion, we would have added to earning power. I mean, why else would we do it?”**
>
> 「那笔 100 亿美元的交易如果做成，会增加盈利能力。不然我们为什么要做？」

**Brevi Panila**（00:52:50）：亲爱的 Warren、亲爱的 Greg，以及各位共同的股东，能在这里非常荣幸。我叫 Brevi Panila，出生在共产时期的阿尔巴尼亚，现在是英国伦敦的一名经济学教师。Warren 和 Charlie 精彩的文字，深刻塑造了我的思考和教学。非常感谢你们这些年给出的诸多洞见。Warren 经常写到 Berkshire 的「盈利能力」（earning power）对股东的重要性。我的问题是：在最近一个财年，你对 Berkshire 盈利能力的估算是什么样的？如果你能谈谈，相对于 Berkshire 报告的净利润指标而言，有哪些重要项目提高了或降低了盈利能力，那就太好了。谢谢。

**Warren Buffett**（00:53:34）：我认为我们的基础盈利能力前一段时间受到了公用事业领域所发生事情的负面影响。我认为我们的盈利能力并没有因为任何大型收购而扩大，但这类收购会周期性出现。所以某个时点我们会看到某个东西……那笔 100 亿美元的交易，如果做成，会增加盈利能力——不然我们为什么要做？所以这非常取决于具体情形，当然也很大程度上取决于大盘在做什么、利率在做什么、市场心理在做什么。我们会在人们最悲观的时候做成最好的交易。从我 1930 年出生起，这就是成立的。我出生时，接下来两年东西变得有吸引力得多，而显然我当时什么也没做。那本来是一生一次的机会，我却因为担心旁边那张婴儿床里的孩子之类的而错过了。在我这一生中，有时候会遇到绝好的机会，它们之所以出现，是因为人就是人。我对各种事情都害怕：我不想当瓦伦达（走钢丝的人），在双子塔之间一条细细的带子上走，诸如此类。但在金融上，我不会因为别人害怕的事情而害怕。比如说，如果 Berkshire 下周跌了 50%，我会把它看作绝好的机会，完全不会困扰我；而大多数人不是这样，他们的反应不同。不是我没有情绪，而是我对股票价格没有情绪。那些判断可以直接进入我的大脑，而情绪会堵在别的地方。所以 Berkshire 会随着时间提高盈利能力：我们留存资金，我们每天在做事情、做决策，人们在做事，或者在留存收益。我们会把盈利能力建起来，但它不会以均匀的节奏出现，也肯定不会在市场价格的上行或下行上逐美元对应。而这正是它成为好生意的原因：投资这门生意，一切都没有被恰当定价，别人越傻，你的机会就越好。好，Becky。

## 英文原文（实录摘录）

**Brevi Panila**（00:52:50）：Dear Warren, dear Greg and dear fellow owners, it’s such a pleasure to be here. My name is Brevi Panila. I was born in Communist Albania, but I’m now teaching economics in London, England. The wonderful writing of Warren and Charlie has significantly shaped my thinking and teaching. I thank you both very much for the many insights over the years. Warren often writes about the importance of Berkshire’s earning power to owners. My question is what was in your estimate of Berkshire’s earning power in the latest fiscal year? It will be great if you can comment on any significant items that either increased or decreased the earning power as compared to the reported net income measures for Berkshire. Thank you.

**Warren Buffett**（00:53:34）：Well, I think our underlying earning power was affected negatively here a while back by what happened in the utility field. I think that our earning power was not enlarged by any large acquisitions that come along, but they come along periodically. So we will see something at some point that… Well, on the one that was 10 billion, we would have added to earning power. I mean, why else would we do it? So that’s very situational, and of course it depends so much on what the general market is doing and what interest rates are doing and what psychology is doing. We will make our best deals when people are the most pessimistic. You know that’s been true ever since I was born in 1930. When I was born, things got much more attractive over the next two years, and apparently, I didn’t do anything about it then. But you know, that was the opportunity of a lifetime, and I blew it by worrying about the kid in the next crib or something. Over my lifetime, you know, I’ve had fabulous opportunities sometimes, and they happen because humans are human. I’m fearful of all kinds of things. I don’t want to try to be one of the Wallendas and walk on a tiny strip between a couple of twin towers or something or whatever it may be, but I don’t get fearful of things that other people get afraid of in a financial way. And you know the idea that if Berkshire went, let’s say Berkshire went down 50% next week, I would regard that as a fantastic opportunity, and then it wouldn’t bother me in the least, and most people aren’t, they just react differently. It’s not that I don’t have emotions, but I don’t have emotions about the prices of stocks. I mean, those decisions get all the way to my brain, whereas emotions can get bogged down somewhere. So Berkshire will increase its earning power over time, as we retain money, I mean, we are doing things, making decisions every day, people are working or retaining earnings. We will build the earning power, but it won’t be coming in any even stream, and it certainly won’t be matched dollar for dollar on either the upside or the downside in market prices, and that’s what makes it a good business, and you know the investment businesses that everything isn’t properly appraised and the sillier other people get, the better your opportunities get. OK, Becky.
