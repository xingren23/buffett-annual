## 问题

你人生中遇到过重大挫折或低谷吗？你是怎么走出来的？

## 回答要点

1. 他先承认这是普遍事实：「每个人都会遇到挫折，有些人运气特别差」，芒格有，他也有，「这一点都不好玩」。
2. 他没有给励志答案：「我没有什么高招能让你在遭遇重大挫折时还觉得人生美好——它就是人生的一部分」。
3. 唯一的确定性是死亡：「你死的时候肯定算一次挫折，所以挫折是每个人都保证会有的」。
4. 他反而强调运气与时代：生在当下的中国或美国，比生在 100 年前、500 年前都好得多，「你抽到了一根好签」。
5. 整体态度是降低自我叙事：运气好的人往往以为是自己的本事。

## 关键引述

> **“Everybody gets setbacks, and some people have particularly bad luck in that respect.”**
>
> 「每个人都会遇到挫折，有些人在这一点上运气特别差。」

> **“It comes with a lifetime. You certainly have a setback when you die.”**
>
> 「这是一辈子的事。你死的时候肯定也算一次挫折。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

**Peter Chan**（02:01:37）：早上好，Buffett 先生、Greg 先生、Ajit 先生。我叫 Peter Chan，来自中国上海。这是我第一次参加股东大会。我想问一个关于人生智慧的问题：您一生中遇到过重大的挫折或低谷吗？您是怎么走过来的、怎么克服的？非常感谢。

**Warren Buffett**（02:02:08）：每个人都会遇到挫折，有些人在这一点上运气特别差，另一些人则只是经历些小挫折。Charlie 有挫折，我也有，这是人生的一部分，而且一点都不好玩。关于「在遭遇重大挫折时如何享受人生」，我没有什么高招给你，但它就是一辈子的事。你死的时候肯定也算一次挫折，所以挫折是每个人都保证会有的。但有些人——我的意思是，这在某种意义上是开不得玩笑的，因为有人运气特别坏，也有人运气特别好。通常运气好的人并不觉得那有多少是运气，而认为是自己的本事。就医疗问题、就人生里各种事情而言，你不太可能碰上那种霉运。你出生在了一个好时代。你把中国历史整个翻一遍，你愿意生在哪个时候？100 年前、500 年前、1000 年前，还是现在？答案明摆着。你是幸运的。如果我祖上二十代都是牧羊人，我想我每天看着那些羊会腻。但你看，我们可以坐在这里，我可以看内布拉斯加（大学）打球——虽然踢的已经不是我们 20 年前踢的那种球了。我意思是，生活里的一切都变得好太多了，你只能认为自己是在娘胎里待了二十万年、然后恰好在正确的时间出来，抽到了一根好签。所以我会把注意力放在我人生中好的那些事上，而不是发生过的坏事。坏事确实会发生，但人生往往可以是美好的。你可能撞上极坏的运气，到目前为止我还没有。但我的一些朋友撞上过，人时不时就会遇上。不过 94 岁了，我想喝什么就能喝什么。他们替我预言过各种可怕的事，但都还没发生。你看看现在的职业橄榄球运动员赚多少，和三四十年相比，你可以说：这不是很好吗？但如果你看职业运动员的寿命，过一阵你就会判断：如果你当初没被第一个选进棒球队、篮球队之类的，也许反而更好。Charlie 和我——我想在某种程度上也代表其他人——我们从来没怎么运动，也没做什么锻炼。我们多年来一直在小心翼翼地保养自己。所以，尽可能地看事物光明的一面。你今天能在这里，已经够幸运了：你身体健康，远道而来，有机会更深入了解一件你感兴趣的事——拿这个和两百年前能给你的处境比一比。好了，说教够了。好，Becky。

## 英文原文（实录摘录）

**Peter Chan**（02:01:37）：Good morning, Mr. Buffett, Mr. Greg, and Mr. Ajit. My name is Peter Chan. I’m from Shanghai, China. This is my first time attending this shareholders’ meeting. I would like to ask a question about the wisdom of life. Have you ever encountered any major setbacks or low points in your life, and how did you get through, and overcome them? Thank you very much.

**Warren Buffett**（02:02:08）：Well, everybody gets setbacks, and some people have particularly bad luck in that respect, and others get through with fairly minor setbacks. Charlie had setbacks. I had setbacks, and it’s part of life, and they’re not fun. I don’t have any great advice for you about having the time of your life while you’re having some major setbacks, but it comes with a lifetime. You certainly have a setback when you die. So everybody’s got setbacks guaranteed to them. But some people get, and I mean, it is no laughing matter in a sense, because people get extraordinary bad luck, and other people get extraordinary good luck. Usually, the people who get good luck don’t really think it was so much luck as themselves. Think that you’re less likely to have it in terms of medical problems. In terms of, you know, various things in life. I mean, you were born at a good time. If you look all the way through the history of China, when would you rather have been born? You know, 100 years ago, 500 years ago, a thousand years ago or now? You know it’s just hands down. You’d be lucky. If I came from 20 generations of shepherds, I think I get kind of tired of my life just looking at these sheep every day. But you know, we can sit here, and I can watch Nebraska. Not quite play the same game of football that we played 20 years ago. But I mean, everything in life has been made so much better that you’ve got to figure that you do a lucky straw by staying in the womb for a couple hundred thousand years, and then just emerging it at the right time. So I would focus on the things that have been good in my life rather than the bad things that have happened. Bad things do happen, but it can often be a wonderful life. You could get terrible breaks in it. So far, that really hasn’t happened. But it happened with some of my friends, but he gets some bad breaks from time to time. But for 94, I’ve been able to drink whatever I want to drink. They predict all kinds of terrible things for me, but it hasn’t happened yet. If you look at what pro football players are making now, and everything compared to what they were making 30 or 40 years ago, you can say, well, isn’t that wonderful? But if you look at the lifespan of professional athletes, after a while, you get to decide that you’re better off if you really weren’t the first one chosen to be on the baseball team or the basketball team, or anything else. Charlie, and I, and I think I speak for the others to some extent, that we never did, we never really exercised that much or did anything. We have been carefully preserving ourselves for years. So look at the bright side of things to the extent that you can. You’re lucky enough you’re here today. You’re healthy, and you come from a long distance, and you’re getting a chance to learn more about something that interests you, and compare that with the situation a couple of hundred years ago that you would have been offered. So anyway, that’s enough moralizing. OK, Becky.
