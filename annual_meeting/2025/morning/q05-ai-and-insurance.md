## 问题

随着 AI 系统能力增强、可解释性下降，它会影响保险公司定价与转移风险的能力吗？这和伯克希尔过去在承保、资本配置上经历过的 disruption 有可比性吗？

## 回答要点

1. Ajit Jain：AI 毫无疑问会是「真正的游戏规则改变者」，会改变风险评估、定价和理赔方式；但**他警惕「花钱追时髦」**，伯克希尔的打法是等机会成型再下手，而不是抢第一。
2. 目前各保险子公司都在小范围试水 AI，还没有形成统一的集团级策略。
3. 巴菲特给出他这一年最著名的表态之一：**「AI 未来十年发展出来的一切，都换不走 Ajit」**——宁可要 Ajit 的判断力，也不要行业里最强的 AI 产品。
4. 隐含判断：在承保这种「长尾、需要判断」的领域，人的经验与克制仍然是稀缺资产。

## 关键引述

> **“There is no question in my mind that AIs are going to be a real game changer, and it's going to change the way we assess risk.”**
>
> 「（Jain）我毫不怀疑 AI 会成为真正的规则改变者，会改变我们评估风险的方式。」

> **“I wouldn't trade everything that's developed in AI in the next 10 years for Ajit.”**
>
> 「我不会用 AI 未来十年发展出的一切去换 Ajit。」

## 中文全文（逐段翻译）

> 与下方「英文原文」逐段对应，按发言顺序排列；术语与专名首次出现时保留英文。

**Becky Quick**（00:47:00）：这个问题来自旧金山的 Sam England，是给 Warren 和 Ajit 的。「随着 AI 系统能力越来越强、可解释性越来越差，你认为它会如何影响保险业评估价格、转移风险的能力？这和 Berkshire 过去在承保上经历过的那些颠覆有可比性吗？」

**Warren Buffett**（00:47:27）：好，Becky。Ajit 的智商比我高大约 100 点，而且他今天上午就在这儿，所以我让他先答。

**Ajit Jain**（00:47:37）：我毫不怀疑 AI 会成为真正的规则改变者，它会改变我们评估风险的方式——价格风险、销售风险，以及最终理赔的方式。话虽如此，我也确实觉得，人们会为追逐下一个时髦玩意儿花掉巨额的钱。我们不太擅长做最快的人、做第一个吃螃蟹的人。我们的做法更像是等到机会成型，那时我们对失败风险、上行和下行会有更好的判断。所以现在，各个保险子公司都在小范围试水 AI，试着弄清楚怎么用最好。但我们还没有做出有意识的、大规模投入的举动，没有往这个机会里砸大钱。我猜我们会保持一种「随时待命」的状态：一旦那个机会冒出来，我们会立刻跳进去。

**Warren Buffett**（00:48:50）：我不会用 AI 未来十年发展出来的一切去换 Ajit。如果给我一个选择：未来十年我有 1000 亿美元可以去参与财产意外险业务；或者拿到市面上任何人做出的最好的 AI 产品，又或者让 Ajit 来做决策——任何时候我都会选 Ajit，这不是开玩笑。好，3 号站。

## 英文原文（实录摘录）

**Becky Quick**（00:47:00）：This question comes from Sam England in San Francisco, and it’s for Warren and Ajit. “As AI systems become more capable and harder to interpret, how do you see that affecting the insurance industry’s ability to assess price, and transfer risk? Are there parallels to past disruptions Berkshire has navigated in underwriting for capital allocation?”

**Warren Buffett**（00:47:27）：OK, Becky. Ajit got about a hundred points of IQ on me, and he’s just going to be here this morning, so I’m going to let him answer the question first.

**Ajit Jain**（00:47:37）：There is no question in my mind that AIs are going to be a real game changer, and it’s going to change the way we assess risk. The price risk, the sell risk, and then the way we end up paying claims. Having said that, I certainly also feel that people end up spending an enormous amount of money trying to chase the next new fashionable thing. We are not very good in terms of being the fastest or the first mover. Our approach is more to wait and see until the opportunity crystallizes, and we have a better point of view in terms of risk of failure, upside, and downside. So right now, individual insurance operations do dabble in AI, and try, and figure out what is the best way to exploit it. But we have not yet made a conscious, big-time effort in terms of pouring a lot of money into this opportunity, and my guess is we will be in a state of readiness, and should that opportunity pop up, we’ll be in a state where we’ll jump in promptly.

**Warren Buffett**（00:48:50）：I wouldn’t trade everything that’s developed in AI in the next 10 years for Ajit. So, if you gave me a choice of having a hundred billion dollars available to participate in the insurance property casualty insurance business for the next 10 years and a choice of getting the top AI product out of whoever’s developing it or having Ajit making the decision, I would take Ajit anytime, and I’m not kidding about that. OK, Station 3.
